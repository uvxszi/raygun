# M46.45 — Playlists, song marks, import targets, transcripts kept, ratings and video sites

## Goal
- **Playlist covers:**
  - Quick access pins of playlists show the playlist's cover as it is now (`PinArt` reads the live playlist), not the picture it had when pinned.
  - A playlist can have a cover of its own: Edit → Change cover (a picture, an address, or one of its songs' covers). It's a `DetailTarget::Playlist` edit (device-local, like M46.30's), shown everywhere a playlist is (`PlaylistSummary::cover_url`, `PlaylistCover coverUrl`).
- **Song marks:** every song row and song card (tables, search, albums, charts, library) shows a check when the song is in Favorites or a playlist and a blue arrow when it's downloaded (`music/SongMarks.tsx`; `collections_saved_keys`).
- **Playlists:**
  - Right-click (or long press) a playlist anywhere (Library, Home, All, the sidebar): Play, Shuffle, Pin, Merge, and Delete, which asks in the menu's place first (`MenuItem.confirm`). Favorites, Downloaded and Local files aren't playlists, so they never offer it.
  - Merge playlists (`MergePlaylistsSheet`, `playlists_merge`): pick two or more in the order their songs should come (numbered, up and down), a name, keep each song once, and keep or remove the merged ones.
  - Song tables get Select all (a toolbar button, the selection bar, Ctrl+A).
- **Import songs:**
  - On Library → Audio (every chip) as a button in the top bar and in its ⋯ menu; no longer in the Playlists list.
  - Save to: a new playlist, an existing one, or Favorites (`ImportTarget`).
  - Find a source: the title and artist can be fixed on the same sheet and searched again; using a source then keeps the fixed names as the song's details.
- **Discography:** each chip (All, Albums, Singles and EPs…) has its own sort: newest, oldest, title, most songs, remembered on the device.
- **Audiobooks:** tapping a chapter plays it (the play button stays). The page says "Audiobook" over the book's title, and a feed without its own picture shows the one the directory listed it with.
- **Transcripts kept:**
  - Switching an episode's captions between its feed transcript and AI keeps the other aside (`captions::move_to` to `<id>#ai` / `<id>#feed`), so switching back is instant and nothing is transcribed twice (`captions_use_ai`, `captions_use_feed`).
  - A song's transcription stays one of its lyrics sources ("Transcribed by AI" in `lyrics_options`).
  - One dropdown under the lyrics (`sound/LyricsSourceMenu.tsx`: the sources' pick, every source's version, the transcription, hear again) and under captions (the show's transcript or AI).
- **Ratings:** a movie's or series' stars say whose they are ("★ 8.7 IMDb"); pressing them lists every addon's ratings with their sites (IMDb and any other a Stremio addon links, `TitleRating`, `stremio_ratings`), each opening its page.
- **Video sites (YouTube, PeerTube):**
  - Their results and title covers are 16:9 (sources with their own Search pill).
  - Opening a video-source title can't spin forever: details and episodes give up after 30 s with an error (`TITLE_TIMEOUT`), and each step is logged.
  - YouTube finds channels and playlists too; each opens as a series of its videos (`channel:<id>`, `playlist:<id>`). A video's comments show under it, a page at a time (`comments` on `video.source`, `video_comments`). Read from Grayjay's YouTube plugin (AGPL-3.0); YouTube 2.3.0.

## Decisions
- **Playlist covers are device-local** like the other detail edits: a picked file can't sync, and a cover is a matter of taste per screen.
- **No per-item cover shapes:** a source with its own pill is a video site, so its covers are 16:9; channel avatars are cropped to fit.
- **The endless loading:** two calls into one extension at once could strand one of them (see Verification); fixed at its root in the host, with the 30 s title timeout kept as a backstop so a source that truly never answers shows an error.

## Verification
- **The endless loading, found:** with the new logs, a YouTube title's fetch never ended; the same calls one at a time worked. Two calls into one extension at once (a title's details and episodes) left the slower one waiting forever: rquickjs polls an extension's pending fetches through whichever call polled last, and that call had finished. Each extension's runtime now has its own driver (`AsyncRuntime::drive`, spawned on the host's `LocalSet`). It also explains Deezer's and MusicBrainz's discographies and Internet Archive searches that "didn't answer in time". Test: `a_call_still_finishes_when_another_into_the_same_extension_ends_first` fails without the driver and passes with it; `video_pills_live` opens YouTube and PeerTube titles with both calls at once.
- Windows (the build before the driver fix): YouTube results 16:9; a playlist's right-click menu (Play, Shuffle, Unpin, Merge, Delete) with the delete question in its place, Cancel kept the playlist; Favorites has no Delete; two test playlists merged in the picked order (B then A) and removed; Change cover set an address and the page and the sidebar pin showed it at once; the merged playlist deleted from the sidebar's menu; a movie's stars read "★ 7.3 IMDb" and open a list with IMDb's link.
- Android: Import from Library → Audio's Import button, Save to Favorites, three songs in their order with saved marks; the discography loads (it timed out before the fix) and Oldest sorts it 1994, 1995…; an audiobook without a feed picture shows the directory's, says "Audiobook", and tapping a chapter plays it; the lyrics dropdown lists the sources' pick, KuGou, LRCLIB and Hear with AI, a pick shows at once and the sources' pick undoes it.
- Live: `video_pills_live` — NASA's channel opens with 30 videos, "NASA Moon Tunes Playlist" with 100, and a video's comments page through (19, then more).
- Tests: `ratings_name_their_sites_and_link_imdb`, `dragOrder.test.ts` (release sorts), Import, TrackTable and SongDownloads tests.
- **Not done:** the installed Windows app wasn't replaced with the fixed build, so the fix wasn't checked there; the fixed `raygun.exe` is built in `target/release`. YouTube's video page with comments was checked through the host (live test), not in the app.
