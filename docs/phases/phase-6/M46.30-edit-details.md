# M46.30 — Edit songs, albums and artists

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):** "allow editing of every album cover image or title of author and editing song names, because sources get stuff wrong so the user can fix these if they want".

## Goal
- **Songs:** the song editor (`SongEditSheet`) gets a **Details** tab beside Sound and Lyrics, also opened by **Edit details** in song menus.
  - Fields: title, artist and album, and a cover: pick an image, paste an address, or go back to the original.
- **Albums:** album pages get **Edit album** (title and cover).
- **Artists:** artist pages get **Edit artist** (a display name and a picture). The page shows them.
- **Edits show everywhere a song does:** saved lists (favorites, playlists), the music library, the queue and the player, Now playing, the miniplayer and the media session, and online lists.
- Every edit has **Reset**, which goes back to what the source said.

## Decisions
- **Overrides, not rewrites:** the source's own data is kept, and edits sit on top. A source that later corrects itself doesn't fight the user's edit, and Reset is always possible.
- **One device-local table**, `local_detail_edit (kind, key, title, artist, album, artwork)` (migration 0026, `raygun-db::detail_edits`). An empty edit deletes its row. Not synced (sync is on hold).
- **Keys:**
  - a song by its resolver track key;
  - an album by its original `artist|album`;
  - an artist by normalized name.
  - Track keys stay the original's, so plays, favorites and downloads keep matching.
- **Rust applies them** (`raygun-app/src/details.rs`, `DetailService`, cached in memory). Each song gets its song edit first, then its album's, then each credited artist's name. The call sites:
  - `collections` saved tracks;
  - `library.track()`;
  - `PlaybackService`'s `with_details` on every enqueue and replace;
  - online lists in commands.
- **Artist pages:** an edited name maps back to the original (`DetailService::original_artist`) for the source lookups behind `music_artist` and `music_artist_info`.
- **Picked covers** are copied to `downloads/edits/covers/<sha256>.<ext>`, stored as `local:<path>` and served through the proxy's `/local/` route (`ImageLinks::local`). Commands: `detail_edit`, `detail_edit_set`, `detail_cover_import` (an upload).
- UI: `music/DetailEditForm.tsx`, `music/useDetailEdit.ts`, the Details tab, and the album and artist sheets.

## Verification
- **Checks:** as M46.29; `search/MusicFinds.test.tsx` edits an album, `details.rs` tests key stability.
- **Windows:**
  - A mix song's menu → Edit details opened the Details tab. The new title showed in the player bar and Now playing as soon as the song played, and Reset brought the source's title back.
  - The mix list itself didn't show the edit. Mixes, genre rows and an artist's popular songs now go through `apply_online` too (`made_for_you_mix`, `music_tag_sources`, `music_artist`).
- **Android (after that fix):**
  - Long-press → Edit details: the edited title shows in the mix list at once.
  - Editing again from the edited name finds the same edit, and Reset restores the original.
  - The editor's title is now "Edit <song>" (it read "…: sound and lyrics" on every tab).
