## Goal
Files you can take out of the app:
- **Save downloads:** finished downloads can also land in the phone's Downloads folder or a folder you pick, as CBZ or a folder of images.
- **Export:** a title (or a selection) as CBZ, PDF or an image folder for manga; EPUB or text for novels; one video file for videos. Chapters that aren't downloaded yet download first, gently.
- **Pages:** save a manga page to Pictures, share it or copy it, from a long press in the reader.

None of the reference apps exports finished titles, so the formats follow the common conventions: CBZ with ComicInfo.xml (Mihon, Komga, Kavita), EPUB 3, and plain PDF.

## Design

### Where files go (the `raygun-audio` plugin, Kotlin)
- **Rust can't write `content://` URIs,** so Kotlin creates the file and hands Rust a file descriptor (`openFileDescriptor(…, "wt").detachFd()`). Rust writes it as a normal `File` in the same process.
- **New plugin calls:**
  - `pickFolder`: the system folder picker (`ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE`). The grant is kept (`takePersistableUriPermission`). Returns the tree URI and its name, or nothing when cancelled.
  - `folderState(uri)`: whether the grant is still there, and the folder's name.
  - `createOutput { place, tree, path, name, mime }`: makes or replaces the file, then returns `{ uri, fd }`.
    - `place` is `downloads` (MediaStore `Download/Raygun/<path>`), `pictures` (`Pictures/Raygun/<path>`) or `tree` (sub-folders made inside the picked folder).
    - A file of the same name in the same place is replaced, not doubled.
    - MediaStore files stay pending until `finishOutput`.
  - `finishOutput(uri, ok)`: publishes the file, or deletes it after a failure.
  - `shareFile(path, mime, title)` and `copyImage(path)`: through the existing FileProvider, from `cache/share/`.
  - **Android 9 and older** (no `RELATIVE_PATH`): `downloads` and `pictures` are plain folders under shared storage, using `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (`maxSdkVersion 28`).
- **Other platforms:** the same calls write under `<data root>/exports/` (desktop isn't built yet; this keeps the code cross-platform).
- **File names:** Mihon's rules (`export/names.rs`):
  - trim dots and spaces at the ends;
  - control characters and `" * / : < > ? \ |` become `_`;
  - at most 240 bytes, cut on a character boundary;
  - `(untitled)` when nothing is left.

### Settings → Downloads (a new settings screen)
- **Save downloads to:** In the app only (default) · Downloads folder · A folder you choose (with Change folder).
  - The app always keeps its own copy, so reading, the proxy and offline playback stay as they are.
  - A finished chapter or episode is then written out too. Removing a download in the app doesn't touch the saved copy.
- **Save chapters as:** CBZ (default) or a folder of images.
- **Saved pages:** one folder per title (`Pictures/Raygun/<Title>/`), on by default.
- **Layout of saved and exported files** (under `Raygun/` in Downloads, or the picked folder):
  - `Manga/<Title>/<Chapter>.cbz`, or `Manga/<Title>/<Chapter>/001.jpg …`;
  - `Novels/<Title>/<Chapter>.txt`;
  - `Videos/<Title>/<Episode>.<ext>`.

### Exports
- **Where:**
  - a title page's top bar (Export);
  - the library selection's actions (Export, one export per title);
  - Downloads → Exports, which lists jobs with progress, Cancel and the result.
- **The Export sheet:**
  - **Chapters:** all; downloaded only; unread; or the range from one chapter to another.
  - **Format:**
    - manga: CBZ (one per chapter), PDF (one file, or one per chapter) or a folder of images;
    - novels: EPUB (one book) or text (one file);
    - videos: the downloaded episodes as video files.
  - **Destination:** the Downloads folder, the picked folder, or Share (one file).
  - **Before starting,** the sheet says how many chapters must download first and their size where known.
- **Jobs** (`ExportService` in `raygun-app/src/export/`):
  - queue missing chapters through the normal download queue, then wait for them;
  - write the files;
  - report progress (`ExportEvent`) and allow cancelling;
  - chapters that failed to download are reported and left out; the rest still export.
  - Jobs aren't kept across restarts: an unfinished job is marked as stopped.
- **Formats:**
  - **CBZ:** the pages stored uncompressed, with ComicInfo.xml (Series, Number, Title, Summary, Writer, Penciller, Translator, Genre, Web, PageCount, LanguageISO). Raygun's own import reads these back.
  - **PDF** (`export/pdf.rs`, written as it goes so big titles don't sit in memory):
    - a page per image, sized to it;
    - JPEG embedded as is;
    - PNG and WebP decoded and stored compressed (with their transparency);
    - other formats (GIF, AVIF, JXL) skipped and counted in the result;
    - pages taller than the PDF limit (200 inches) are split.
    - One PDF per title has a bookmark per chapter.
  - **EPUB 3:** the cover, a table of contents, one XHTML file per chapter built from the text blocks (headings, paragraphs, separators). Chapter images are left out (they aren't downloaded).
  - **Text:** UTF-8 with a heading per chapter.
  - **Video:**
    - a plain download is copied;
    - an HLS download is joined into one file (`.ts`, or `.mp4` for fMP4 segments), with AES-128 segments decrypted using the saved keys;
    - a stream whose audio is a separate rendition is refused with a reason;
    - downloaded subtitles are saved next to it with the same name.

### Pages (the manga reader)
- **A long press on a page** opens a sheet: Save page, Share page, Copy page.
- **The page's bytes** come from the download, else the page cache, else a fetch with the page's headers.
- **Saved** as `Pictures/Raygun[/<Title>]/<Title> - <Chapter> - <n>.<ext>`. A notice offers Share.

### Being gentle with sources
- **Retries:** chapter downloads retry a page three times with backoff (2, 4, 8 s) on network errors, HTTP 429 and 5xx, honouring `Retry-After` (up to a minute).
- **Resume:** pages already saved are kept when a chapter fails or is paused, and the next attempt fetches only what's missing.
- **One chapter at a time,** two pages in flight (unchanged). Exports use the same queue, so a big export can't hammer a source.
- **Page names** are padded to the page count (`0001.jpg` past 999 pages), so order holds.

## Decisions
- **Research (2026-11-01, over `reference/`):** none of the cloned apps exports finished chapters or titles. Mihon's CBZ is only its download format, Aniyomi's only "export" is its `.mkv` download, and Shosetsu has just a plan for EPUB import. So the formats follow common conventions: CBZ with Anansi ComicInfo.xml (Mihon, Komga, Kavita), EPUB 3 with an EPUB 2 NCX, and plain PDF.
- **How files reach shared storage:**
  - Kotlin makes each file and passes Rust a detached file descriptor. This is the pattern Mihon uses for its native zip writer.
  - MediaStore needs no permission on Android 10+. A picked folder uses the Storage Access Framework with a kept grant.
  - Android 11+ refuses the storage root and `Download/` itself in the picker, so a sub-folder has to be picked or made.
  - A file with the same name in the same place is replaced, not doubled.
- **The app keeps its own copy.** Saving outside the app is a copy after the download finishes.
  - Reading, the proxy and offline playback keep plain file access.
  - Reading back from a picked folder (and dropping the app's copy) is M49's storage screen.
- **ZIP writing:**
  - streaming (`ZipWriter::new_stream`) when the descriptor can't seek;
  - pages stored uncompressed, ComicInfo.xml deflated;
  - EPUB's `mimetype` first and stored.
- **PDF:** hand-written and streaming (no PDF crate).
  - JPEG frames embed as they are; Adobe CMYK JPEGs get an inverted `Decode`.
  - PNG (`png`) and WebP (`image-webp`, pure Rust) are decoded and stored with Flate; alpha becomes a soft mask only when some pixel isn't opaque.
  - A page wider than 200 inches is scaled down. Taller ones are split into pages that each show a slice of one image object.
  - Text strings are UTF-16BE hex, so any script works.
- **EPUB:** the cover goes in the metadata only (no cover page in the spine), since Raygun's own import and some readers take a non-linear cover page for a chapter. Chapter images are left out, because they aren't downloaded with the text.
- **Video:**
  - HLS downloads are joined as their saved playlist lists the parts.
  - AES-128 segments are decrypted with the saved key, using the IV given or the sequence number (RFC 8216 §5.2).
  - Part names must be plain file names (no `/`, `\` or `..`).
  - Separate audio renditions are refused in the plan, with the reason shown.
- **Exports wait for downloads through the normal queue** (one chapter at a time).
  - The job checks the queue every second.
  - A chapter that fails, or is taken off the queue, is left out and counted.
  - A paused queue shows "Downloads are paused" on the job.
- **Novel text** comes from `NovelService::chapter`, so downloaded chapters and imported EPUB books are read the same way the reader reads them.
- **Jobs live in memory** for the session (the last 30 finished ones are kept). A save after a download only shows in the list when it fails.
- **Sharing** sends one file. A plan that would make several files says so, and the sheet keeps Export off.
- **Page actions:**
  - the page is fetched back through the proxy, which has the download, the page cache and the source's headers;
  - only the proxy's own `/image/` and `/local/` URLs are accepted;
  - shared and copied pages go through the cache's `share/` folder (emptied each time) and the existing FileProvider.
- **Gentler downloads:**
  - three retries (2, 4, 8 s, or `Retry-After` up to 60 s) on network errors, 429 and 5xx; a 429/503 with `Retry-After` is `ImageError::Busy`;
  - pages are written as `.tmp` then renamed, so a cut-off write isn't taken as saved;
  - a `.pages` mark (a hash of the page list) keeps a partial folder only when the source still lists the same pages;
  - removing a chapter deletes its partial folder too;
  - page names are padded to the page count, at least three digits as before.
- **Settings → Downloads** is a settings screen of its own (`SavePrefs`, one `save` row).
- **Library tabs** (added mid-milestone, committed as d28c6d4): Audio, Comics, Books and Videos.
  - Podcasts is a chip of Audio, and `media=podcast` links still open it.
  - Search, Browse and the podcast page keep naming each kind (Music, Manga, Novels), through `library.media.*`; the tabs use `library.tabs.*`.

## Verification
- **Checks:**
  - `pnpm lint`, `pnpm type-check` and `pnpm test`: 292 UI tests, among them `export/Export.test.tsx` (the Export sheet's plan, range, share rule and start; Downloads → Exports with progress, cancel, results and dismiss; library selection export; Settings → Downloads; a lost folder; the reader's page sheet);
  - `cargo test --workspace`: file names; CBZ with ComicInfo read back by the comic import; PDF structure with every cross-reference offset checked; EPUB read back by the EPUB import; HLS joining with AES-128 and IVs from the sequence number; downloads that resume and retry with `Retry-After`;
  - `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`;
  - bindings current.
- 📱 **On the Pixel_4 emulator:**
  - **Folder:** Settings → Downloads → Choose a folder opened the system picker. `Documents/RaygunTest` was granted, shown as "RaygunTest", and set as the place to save.
  - **Saving a download:** Chainsaw Man chapter 97 (MangaDex) downloaded and appeared in the folder as `Manga/Chainsaw Man/0097 Chapter 97_ Love, Love, Chainsaw.cbz`: 23 stored pages and a full ComicInfo (series, number, summary, writer, penciller, the scanlation group, genres and link).
  - **Export with downloads first:**
    - Chapters 96–97 as one PDF to the Downloads folder; the sheet said "2 chapters, 1 file(s). 1 chapter downloads first".
    - Chapter 96 downloaded (and was saved to the folder as CBZ too). The job finished as `Download/Raygun/Manga/Chainsaw Man.pdf`: 42 pages, 133 objects, every offset correct, bookmarks "Chapter 96: This Flavor" and "Chapter 97: Love, Love, Chainsaw.".
    - Android's PDF viewer opened it, each page at its image's size.
  - **Novel:** Frankenstein (Project Gutenberg) to EPUB in the folder. All 28 chapters downloaded first. The book had its cover, author, 28 spine items and a table of contents. Each downloaded chapter was also saved as text in `Novels/<title>/`, as the setting asks.
  - **Video:**
    - an AES-128 HLS episode from the local test addon was downloaded with the show in the library;
    - the save after the download wrote `Videos/Raygun Test Show/Raygun Test Show - S1 E1 · Pilot.ts` (8.3 MB, all 44,232 packets with the MPEG-TS sync byte, so decrypted) plus its subtitles;
    - the Export sheet then wrote the same to `Download/Raygun/Videos/`.
  - **Pages:**
    - a long press in the reader opened Save, Share and Copy;
    - Save wrote `Pictures/Raygun/Chainsaw Man/Chainsaw Man - Chapter 1_ A Dog and a Chainsaw - 1.jpg` and said where;
    - Copy said "Copied";
    - Share opened the system share sheet ("Sharing image").
  - **Library tabs:** Audio · Comics · Books · Videos, with Podcasts as an Audio chip.
  - **Clean-up:** the test folder, saved files, pages and the addon were removed, and app data was cleared.
- **Bugs found on the device and fixed:**
  - the range pickers overflowed the sheet side by side (now stacked, each truncating);
  - chapters whose text starts with their own title showed it twice in EPUB and text;
  - Android added ".txt" to subtitle files saved as `text/plain` (now a generic type);
  - the podcasts view sat 4 px lower than the other Audio chips.
- **Not seen on the device:**
  - CBZ export to a folder per chapter (images), and PDF per chapter;
  - text export;
  - Share for a whole PDF;
  - a lost folder grant;
  - Android 9's plain-folder path;
  - retries against a real 429 (covered by tests).
