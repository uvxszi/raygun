# M46.24 — Player and shell polish

## Goal
- **Cards don't get cut:**
  - The side panel's Now playing column is a scrolling flex column, and the cards (lyrics, artist, credits) clip their overflow. When the column ran out of room, they shrank and lost their last lines ("Performed by" and the padding under it).
  - Cards and the column now keep their full height (`shrink-0`) and scroll instead.
- **Lyrics card sizes to what it has:**
  - Desktop panel: 26 rem tall only when there are lyrics. While loading, with none, or after an error, it's a slim card: the heading, one line of status, and Add lyrics.
  - The phone's card under the full player shows the same slim form when there's nothing to show.
- **No "Show video" overlay on the artwork.** The video toggle beside the title stays: side panel, full player, player bar.
- **Close the player:**
  - Phone: swiping the player bar down stops playback and empties the queue. The bar slides away, following the finger.
  - Desktop: a small × at the right of the seek bar, after the length, does the same.
  - It's the queue's Clear. Last played saves the empty queue, so nothing reopens at the next start.
- **Next in queue grows:**
  - The side panel's "Next in queue" card has a drag handle on its lower edge. Dragging sets how many songs it shows, one row at a time: 1 by default, up to 10.
  - The count is kept in `Settings::panels` (`next_up_rows`).
- **Interface size:**
  - Desktop already has Settings → Appearance → Window → Zoom (50–200 %) with Ctrl+= / Ctrl+− / Ctrl+0.
  - Ctrl+scroll now zooms in steps of 10 %, except in the readers, which zoom their pages with it.
  - The numpad's + and − work like = and −.
  - Phones get the same setting as **Interface size** in Appearance. It scales the root font size, so text and spacing grow and layouts stay intact. The webview zoom isn't available on Android.
- **Mascot button:**
  - A round button left of the bell in the desktop bar (and left of search in the phone's Home bar), drawn as an anime girl's face in the same line style as the other icons.
  - It opens an attached popover: "Mascots are coming soon", with one line on what they'll do.

## Decisions
- **Closing is clearing, not hiding.** A hidden bar with a queue still playing would leave sound with no controls; Spotify's and YouTube Music's phone bars dismiss by stopping. The swipe needs a real downward drag (40 px or a flick) so a tap or a sideways swipe never closes it.
- **Next-up rows, not pixels:** rows keep the card tidy at any zoom. The handle snaps to whole rows.
- **Phones scale `font-size`:** Tailwind sizes and spacing are in rem, so 90–130 % works without breaking full-height layouts the way CSS `zoom` would. Icons in px stay as they are.
- **The mascot icon** is an inline SVG (`MascotIcon` in `shell/MascotButton.tsx`): 24×24, a 2 px round stroke like Lucide's, with a fringe, two eyes and twin tails, so it sits with the bell and download icons.

## Verification
- **Tests:**
  - the side panel's Next in queue: one song by default, the handle's arrow keys add a row, saved as `nextUpRows`;
  - a lyrics card without lyrics isn't 26 rem tall;
  - the × after the seek bar clears the queue;
  - swipes: down (40 px or a flick) closes;
  - Ctrl and the wheel zoom (plain scrolling doesn't);
  - the mascot's popover.
  - `PanelPrefs` validation; all app tests (441), clippy, `cargo test --workspace`.
- **Windows (release build):**
  - The side panel kept its cards whole: Credits showed its name and "Performed by" with the card's padding under it.
  - The lyrics card was one line ("No lyrics found for this track", Add lyrics).
  - Dragging Next in queue's edge down showed five songs.
  - The mascot button sat left of the bell; its popover said mascots are coming.
  - Ctrl+= zoomed to 110 % and Ctrl+0 back. Ctrl+scroll didn't change the zoom when sent by the automation tool. Whether that tool holds Ctrl during a scroll couldn't be confirmed, so this needs a check by hand; the handler's test passes.
  - The × after the seek bar closed the player: the bar and the side panel went away.
- **Android (debug build):**
  - Home's bar has the mascot button left of Search; it opens the "coming soon" sheet.
  - Swiping the player bar down closed it.
