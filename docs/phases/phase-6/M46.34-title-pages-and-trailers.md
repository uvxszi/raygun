# M46.34 — Movie and series pages: content up top, trailers that play

## Goal
- **The first screen holds what matters:**
  - The backdrop is at most about 55 % of the window's height, fading into the page.
  - On it sit the poster, title, year, length, rating, genres, Play (with Resume), Add to library, Mark watched, Download and Trailer.
  - The description, cast and episodes follow right under it, not a screen below.
- **Trailers play by themselves:**
  - When a title has a trailer (YouTube), it starts muted in a small rounded window on the backdrop's right side, faded at its edges.
  - Its controls: play/pause, sound on/off, stop (closes the window) and full screen, which opens the existing trailer overlay.
  - It stops when Play is pressed or the page is left.
  - **Settings → Player → Trailers on title pages** (on by default) turns it off. The Trailer button still works.

## Decisions
- **The embed is the same one as trailers use** (`video/YouTubeOverlay.tsx`'s host page), started muted because webviews block autoplay with sound.
- **Phones get the same window**, under the title instead of over the backdrop, since a phone's backdrop is small.

## Verification
- Windows: Night of the Living Dead (Cinemeta) showed its trailer muted in a small window on the backdrop, with pause and stop, and the title, facts and Play below it.
- Tests: the Watch title tests (one Play button on phones, the inline one on wide windows).
