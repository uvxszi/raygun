# M46.46 — Downloads that keep trying, recordings, lyrics kept, covers kept

## Goal
- **Downloads keep trying** (Kreate's way, read in `reference/Kreate`: Media3's `DownloadManager` with `minRetryCount`, a network requirement, and a stream checked before it's used, else fetched another way):
  - A stream that drops or answers 5xx/429/408 is tried again (three times, 1 s, 3 s, 8 s, or the server's `Retry-After`), continuing from the bytes it has; a stream that stalls for 30 s counts as dropped.
  - A source's own stream that fails falls back to the other sources instead of failing the song; ten candidates are tried, not six.
  - A song that failed for network reasons goes back to the queue by itself after 30 s, 2 min and 10 min.
- **Downloads in lists:** songs queued or downloading show a small animated arrow beside their title (`SongMarks`).
- **Selections:** Download in the selection bar skips songs already saved or on their way ("Download 3"); the Select all button selects everything, and pressed again clears the selection and closes the bar.
- **Recording:** a recording opens its own window over the app (the song, its progress, Cancel). It pauses what was playing, plays the song from its start trim to its end trim, and the player can't be paused, sought, skipped or changed while it records (Rust refuses those controls). When it ends the song leaves the queue and the player stays paused. A recording is judged against the trimmed length.
- **Import albums:** the album comes from what the sources agree on: an imported album no source names (a misread screenshot) gives way to the matches' album, spelled as the sources spell it.
- **Lyrics:**
  - None found: asked again with the core title, the first artist, and no album or length.
  - Several songs selected → Transcribe lyrics: heard one after another, in order.
  - Every lyrics found is kept on the device (`local_lyrics_cache`), shown at once next time and offline; transcriptions stay as they are (captions store, the song's lyrics).
  - The lyrics menu has Delete saved lyrics; Settings → Data → Storage has Lyrics: how many are kept, Clear for all songs or only songs not in the library, Favorites, playlists or downloads, and transcriptions apart.
  - Lyrics not shown for 30 days (0 = never) are forgotten by a daily sweep, except for Favorites, downloaded songs and local files (each a switch, on by default). `Settings::lyrics_cache` (`LyricsCachePrefs`).
- **Covers kept:** covers picked in Edit details (from a source or an address) and playlist covers show through the cover cache as kept covers; the sweep also keeps saved songs' covers and the covers found for them from other sources (M46.40).
- **Playlist Edit:** a full pill button that opens its options in a popover beside it (rename, change cover, move and remove buttons on the rows, delete).
