# M46.40 — What's already known shows at once; screenshots in order

## Goal
- **Cached first, everywhere a page or list can start from something already seen:**
  - Comic, book and video title pages: the list's title and cover (done in M46.38 with `manga/knownCard.ts`).
  - **Find in other sources:** matches already in any cached search or source page (any query) show at once, marked "seen in search"; the live searches then fill in and replace them.
  - Album, playlist and artist pages: the album's (or playlist's) title, artist, cover and year from the search, chart or discography it was opened from, while its songs load or when the source fails.
  - Stremio title pages: the catalogue card's name, poster and year.
  - Podcast and audiobook pages opened from search: the result's title, author and art while the feed loads.
  - One helper finds them: `shell/queryMemory.ts` (`findInCache(client, prefix, match)`), so new pages get it for free.
- **Screenshots in order:**
  - Choosing screenshots no longer reads them at once. They show as a numbered list of thumbnails, sorted by when they were taken (file time), with up/down, remove and **Add more**.
  - **Read N screenshots** reads them in that order. Songs repeated across overlapping screenshots are kept once (as before).

## Verification
- Windows: a YouTube result opened with its title and 16:9 thumbnail from the list while the page loaded; Reload sits beside Back and Forward; the song and playlist menus have one copy item each.
- Android: Search → Music showed an artist picture found through another source.
- Tests: `ScreenshotQueue.test.tsx` (taken order, move, remove, read in order), Collections and Import tests, `cover_fallback` key test.
- Not checked by hand this round: reading several real screenshots on a device (covered by the queue test) and a menu scrolled while open.
