# M46.57: Raygun 2.0.0

Status: planned. Starts once the raygun-app split (M46.56) is in main; the areas below are worked
in parallel, one agent each, in the new crates where they exist.

## 1. Smart Chips (new)

Smart Chips are optional features a person installs, like an extension: listed with what they do
and their size, downloaded, then turned on. The top bar's Smart Chips button (now "coming soon",
`shell/SmartChipsButton.tsx`) opens their list.

- **Framework** (`raygun-app-base::chips`): a catalogue (`chips/catalog.json`, files pinned by
  SHA-256 like `raygun-ai`'s models, with `id`, `name`, `description`, `size`, `files`, `kind`),
  `ChipsService` (install = download + check + unpack under `chips/<id>/`, remove, on/off, state
  events), `chips::enabled(app, ChipId)` for code that a chip gates, `Settings::chips` (on/off per
  chip, synced; what's installed is device-local). Commands `chips_list`, `chips_install`,
  `chips_remove`, `chips_set_enabled`; `ChipsEvent`. UI: the Smart Chips sheet becomes a list
  (install with progress, cancel, on/off, remove), and a Smart Chips page in Settings.
- **Downloadable**: every chip has a package even when its code ships in the app, so later chips
  (and mascots) can carry models and data; a chip's features show only while it is installed and
  on.
- **Smart Shuffle** (the first chip, Spotify's equivalent): with it on, the shuffle button cycles
  off → shuffle → Smart Shuffle → off. Smart Shuffle shuffles the list and adds recommended songs to
  it, about one for every three of the list's own, chosen from the list's songs: `music.recommend`
  sources (similar artists, radio), then the list's artists' other popular songs, then Made for
  You's taste model; songs already in the list, hidden artists, explicit songs while those are off,
  and unavailable songs are left out. Added songs carry a mark (a sparkle) in the queue and the
  player, with "Add to this playlist" and "Not for me" (removes it and never suggests it for that
  list again). Turning Smart Shuffle on again refreshes the suggestions. Works offline with
  downloaded songs only. `PlaybackCore` keeps the added songs apart from the list's own (Connect
  carries them).
- **Smart Subtitles** (chip): see 3.
- **Smooth Motion** becomes a chip: its shaders move into the chip's package (Android and desktop
  load them from the chip's folder); the Effects sheet shows Smooth motion only while the chip is
  installed and on. Existing per-title settings stay.

## 2. Importing songs

- Unlimited songs: no 100 cap on pasted lists, CSV or screenshots. Spotify links: the embed page
  gives at most 100 songs; for longer playlists Raygun uses Spotify's official Web API when the
  person has added their own client ID and secret in Settings → Import (client credentials,
  documented endpoints only, paged), and otherwise says the list was cut at 100 and how to get all
  of it (an Exportify CSV, or the keys). Never undocumented endpoints.
- Screenshots: while reading, a progress line per screenshot (preparing, reading text, rows found)
  and overall. The review sheet can't be closed by tapping outside it (only its own buttons, Back
  asks first). In review, tapping a row's cover (or the screenshot it came from) opens the picture
  to inspect it, zoomable; there a screenshot can be cropped (and read again) before matching.
- Text readers (OCR): after downloading the text reader it works without reloading the app (the
  "The requested file could not be read…" error: the reader opened the model while it was still
  being written or through a handle taken before the download moved it; open it fresh after
  install). More readers, chosen in Settings → AI → On-device models / Import: PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5
  mobile (detection + recognition, ONNX, on-device; through the runtime already used, or `ort`),
  the AI providers the person has set up that read pictures (Claude, OpenAI-compatible vision
  models: they're asked for title/artist rows directly, the best accuracy), Windows OCR on Windows,
  ocrs as now. A "Try another reader" button in review reads the same screenshots again with a
  different one. Measure each on the screenshots in the tests' fixtures and keep the numbers in
  this file.

  Measured (`screenshot_accuracy`, `crates/raygun-app-music/tests/fixtures/screenshots`, drawn by
  its `make.py`: a light phone list, a dark one, a desktop table, a Japanese list; songs read
  with title and artist right, release build on a desktop CPU):

  | Reader | Light (10) | Dark (8) | Table (8) | Japanese (6) | Total | Time a picture |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 mobile (rten) | 10 | 8 | 8 | 6 | 32/32 | 0.3–0.5 s |
  | Windows OCR (English profile) | 10 | 8 | 8 | 0 | 26/32 | 0.1–0.4 s |
  | ocrs | 9 | 6 | 7 | 0 | 22/32 | 0.2–0.3 s |

  AI providers' vision models aren't measured here (they need the person's key); they're asked
  for rows directly (`raygun_ai::vision`), so title/artist pairing doesn't depend on the layout.
  The reload bug: the files picked in the WebView were read only at upload time, through a
  reference to the picked document that can stop reading on Android ("The requested file could
  not be read…"); screenshots are now copied into memory when picked (`keepInMemory`), and every
  read loads its models from their files (`ocr::Engine::load`), nothing cached from before an
  install.

## 3. Video sources and subtitles

- **Picking** (`video_session*`, `video_quality`, `stremio`): judge a source by more than quality.
  A link plays only if it really plays the title: its length must be near the others' (the median
  of the working sources' lengths, or the title's runtime from its details); far shorter links are
  placeholders ("this video isn't available" clips), trailers or samples and are passed over
  (no false positives: only when several sources agree, or the runtime is known). A source that
  plays well stays: a later one replaces it only when clearly better by length match, health and
  then quality, and only early (as now); a source that worked before for the title is kept. When
  a picked source fails, go on to the next ones in ranking order, never back to one already left;
  sources that take long still join and can be picked.
- **Sources tab toggles** (Sources sheet and Settings → Video player): Leave out trailers, Leave
  out short videos (under a share of the median, default on). Each source row has a button opening
  its settings: turn it off (for this title or everywhere), its addon's or extension's settings.
- **Subtitles from other sources**: fix what stops them showing (subtitles from addons that aren't
  the one playing, from stream providers, from torrent files). **Delay**: the delay applies to
  what's on screen at once and keeps working past the end and before the start (negative delays,
  delays longer than the video), on both players; the timing follows the playhead exactly after
  seeks and speed changes.
- **Smart Subtitles** (chip): with it on, every subtitle and audio track is compared with the
  others of the same language from other sources (cue timings and text similarity, a quick
  algorithm: normalized words per time window, Jaccard/time-aligned overlap); the ones most like
  the rest go first and get a "Matching" tag, ones unlike all others a "Might not match" note.
  Audio tracks are compared by length and silence pattern where cheap. A toggle in the subtitles
  sheet (only while the chip is installed and on).

## 4. Sound and picture

- **Pitch, tempo and equalizer for any audio**: the music player, podcasts, audiobooks and the
  video player get tempo (speed without pitch change, as now for speech), pitch (semitones, without
  tempo change) and the equalizer (the music player's bands; videos get their own preset per title
  or follow the app's). Android: Media3 `SonicAudioProcessor` (speed/pitch) + the existing sound
  processors; desktop: Web Audio (`playbackRate` with `preservesPitch`, a pitch shifter worklet).
- **Watermark remover** (video effect): the person draws a box around a watermark (or Raygun finds
  the static bright/transparent overlay that stays put over many frames); inside it the effect
  removes the overlay: estimating its alpha mask from the frames (static over changing video), then
  inverting the blend, with brightness/contrast/feather controls for leftovers. Real time on the
  GPU, both players, off by default; its region is per title.
- **Fx button**: the player's effects button shows "Fx" instead of the sliders icon.
- **Connect in the phone's full player**: a Devices button left of the lyrics button.

## 5. Devices

- **Syncing files with linked devices**: choose what to send to another device: downloaded songs,
  chapters and episodes, picked covers, edited lyrics and timings, song edits (trims, volume).
  Over the device link's channel (the sync connection's stream, resumable, one file at a time),
  with progress and a per-kind switch in Devices. Song edits and lyrics edits sync as data where
  they're tables; files go as files.
- **Barcode scanning**: the scanner (`qrScanner.ts`) also reads EAN/UPC/ISBN barcodes. Search's
  scan button: a CD's or record's barcode finds its album (MusicBrainz release by barcode), a
  book's ISBN finds the book (Open Library, then the book sources), a film's disc its title where a
  source can; Android through the camera, desktop through a picture or a webcam.

## 6. Web

- Raygun on the web can't add sources by address: Sources → Add must work in the browser (the
  page reads repository indexes and addon manifests itself where CORS allows, and through a linked
  app otherwise).

## 7. Sizes and icons

- At other interface sizes and window sizes many icons look wrong (blurry, clipped, misaligned or
  out of proportion with their buttons). Go through the app at interface sizes 75/100/125/150 %
  and text sizes, phone and desktop, and fix how icons scale (`data-ui-scaled`, `--ui-scale`,
  lucide sizes, the top bar, player bar, sheets, the native Android player's icons with the app's
  size).
