# M46.54 YouTube playback: SponsorBlock, original audio, H.264

NouTube's player improvements (nonbili/NouTube, AGPL-3.0) for Raygun's own YouTube playback:
songs played in YouTube's embed or as a video's audio file, YouTube videos and trailers in YouTube's
player in Raygun's window, song downloads, and the phone's video player.

## SponsorBlock

- `raygun-app/src/sponsorblock/` (`SponsorBlock`, managed as `Arc<SponsorBlock>`): lookups by the
  first 4 characters of the video id's SHA-256 (`GET sponsor.ajay.app/api/skipSegments/<prefix>`,
  every category Raygun handles, action type `skip`); only the video's own parts are kept. 404 is
  "none". Kept a day in memory (256 videos) and on disk (`cache/sponsorblock/<hash>.json`, none
  written while incognito), swept after 30 days or past 5000 files. Offline Mode asks nothing and
  uses what's kept however old; Data Saver asks only for what plays (`Want::Now`) and keeps using
  what it has; a failed lookup falls back to kept parts.
- Settings: `Settings::youtube` (`YouTubePrefs`: `sponsor_block`, `original_audio`), one row.
  `SponsorBlockPrefs`: on by default; per category Off, Skip or Show only (sponsor, selfpromo,
  interaction: skip; intro, outro, preview: show; music_offtopic: skip; filler: off);
  `trim_downloads` (off). Parts marked on a video more than 2 s longer or shorter than the one
  playing are left out (`Segment::fits`).
- Songs: `PlaybackService` keeps a `LoadSkips` per load of a YouTube video (the embed's
  `MediaSource::YouTube`, a resolver pick that is a YouTube video's audio, a downloaded copy whose
  origin is one). Progress reports reaching a skipped part seek past it and the parts that follow
  on (`SkippedSegmentEvent`, a toast with Undo → `playback_undo_skip`, which plays the part and
  doesn't skip it again in that load). A part where a load starts, known already, is passed at
  once. The seek bar marks the parts (`SkipSegmentsEvent`, `playback_skip_segments`,
  `youtube/SkipMarks.tsx`).
- YouTube's player in Raygun's window (`video/YouTubeOverlay.tsx`, `video/TrailerWindow.tsx`):
  `sponsorblock_segments(videoId, durationMs)`; the same rules in TS (`youtube/skipParts.ts`); the
  overlay notes skips with Undo and passes the parts to the embed host page, whose player script
  draws them on YouTube's own seek bar.
- Downloads: with `trim_downloads`, a downloaded M4A of a YouTube video loses its
  `music_offtopic` parts (`music_downloads/trim.rs`, the remux's `Source::Cut`, packets copied);
  the file is named `*.trimmed.m4a` and its other parts are no longer skipped. Other files keep
  their parts, which are skipped as they play.

## Original audio and codecs

- `raygun-ext-host::youtube::pick_format` prefers the original track of a video with dubbed ones
  (track name "original", or `xtags` `acont=original`; dubbed = `acont=dubbed*` or
  `isAutoDubbed`; audio descriptions never), unless `set_original_audio(false)`.
- YouTube's embed: the host page's address carries `audio=original|default` and
  `codec=auto|h264` (`youtube_embed::url`), passed as player parameters into YouTube's frame,
  where `youtube_embed/player.js` (an all-frames initialization script, idle elsewhere) picks the
  original audio track and steers codecs: `h264` refuses VP8/VP9/AV1/WebM in `canPlayType`,
  `isTypeSupported` and `decodingInfo` (h264ify); `auto` refuses VP9/AV1 only where the webview
  reports no power-efficient decoder.
- `VideoPlayerPrefs::video_codec` (Auto, H264): the phone's player sets Media3's preferred video
  MIME type to H.264.
