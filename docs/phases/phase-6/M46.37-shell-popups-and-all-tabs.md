# M46.37 — Shell: popups, All tabs, Your music, small fixes

## Goal
- **Popups from the desktop bar** (`shell/BarPopovers.tsx`):
  - Updates (the bell) opens a popover beside it with the latest new chapters and episodes (8) and **Open Updates**.
  - Downloads opens one with songs, chapters and videos together, those under way first, and **Open Downloads**.
  - Phones keep opening the pages.
- **All tabs, first and default:**
  - Library: **All** · Audio · Comics · Books · Videos (`library/AllLibrary.tsx`): a row per visible kind, in the layout's order (Audio: Favorites, playlists, followed podcasts; the others read or watched lately first), each with See all. Empty everywhere, it says so and links to Browse → Search. All is a layout entry like the others (`LAYOUT_LIBRARY_MEDIA`).
  - Downloads: All shows songs, chapters, videos and exports one after another.
  - Updates: All shows chapters, then episodes.
- **Your music** on Home:
  - Favorites, Downloaded and Local files.
  - Then **what was played from lately**: albums, playlists, mixes, artists, charts, genres, podcasts and audiobooks, newest first.
  - Then playlists not already there, and a row of the **latest favorites** that plays them.
- **Categories in More:** "Group your comics, books and videos".
- **"Nothing to pick up yet":** a Home section of its own (`getStarted`, first) with ×, which hides it in the layout, so Edit Home brings it back. It shows only while nothing is under way: no history (every play is recorded, podcasts and books included), nothing being watched, no comic or book started.
- **Panel resizing:** a panel let go keeps its size at once: the saved size goes into the settings cache before the save (`usePanelSizes`), so nothing jumps back for a frame.
- **Covers seen once show at once:** pictures this run already showed load eagerly and decode before drawing (`shell/imageMemory.ts`), instead of popping in again when a tab comes back. The proxy already lets the webview cache them for a day.

## Decisions
- **Where plays come from is told by the page, not stored with history:**
  - Pages that play (album, library album, playlist, mix, artist, chart, genre, podcast, audiobook) say what they are (`music/playContext.ts`, `usePlayContext`).
  - The central play calls (`playResults`, `playCollection`, `playFromLibrary`, podcast and audiobook plays) remember it through `recent_contexts_add`.
  - Rust keeps 24, device-local (`local_meta` `music.recentContexts`), like history's order. No migration, and plays from search or Home rows aren't counted as places.
- **History stays one list:** `/watch/history` is videos only and Home's Recently played covers the rest, so neither needed an All tab.

## Verification
- Windows: the Downloads popover listed three songs with their state and Open Downloads; Library's tabs read All, Audio, Comics, Books, Videos; Home's Your music showed the Latest favorites row; More → Categories reads "Group your comics, books and videos".
- Android: Library opened on All (empty state with "Find something in Browse"; then a Books row after adding a book).
- Tests: AppBar (Downloads popover → Open Downloads), Library tabs, Layout (`all` entry), the getStarted section (Rust layout test).
