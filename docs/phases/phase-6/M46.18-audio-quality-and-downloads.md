# M46.18 — Audio quality and downloads

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):**
- "add an Audio Quality setting dropdown in settings and also separate audio quality downloads and for when on cell data, like spotify, but quality based on what you get";
- "in library also add new block for downloaded stuff, it doesnt have to be in favorites or playlist, add a button next to the + icon to download a music or something else and gets added to downloads and can be undownloaded but show a small attached popup to confirm to undownlaod";
- "for downloads page, add option to save as mp3, and on the Where section also allow select folder now instead of going to settings".

## Goal
- **Audio quality** (Settings → Player → Audio quality): three dropdowns, as Spotify has them.
  - Streaming, Mobile data and Downloads.
  - Each offers Low (~64 kbps), Normal (~128), High (~192), Very high (~320) and Best available (lossless included).
  - Defaults: High, Normal, Very high (`Settings::audio_quality`, added in M46.16).
- **Quality steers the stream pick** ("based on what you get"):
  - The resolver aims for the setting: streaming, or mobile data while an Android phone is on a cellular network; downloads use their own.
  - `music.stream` providers get `targetKbps` (`null` = the best there is) and may report each candidate's `bitrateKbps`. A bitrate in the quality label ("320 kbps", "FLAC") counts too.
  - Among matches as good as the best one, the nearest bitrate plays. Quality never beats a better match.
  - A remembered pick is used again only if its bitrate suits the quality wanted now.
  - Internet Archive (1.3.0) picks the item's format nearest the target: 64/128 kbps MP3, VBR MP3 or FLAC.
- **Download button beside +** (wide player bar, side panel, full player): downloads the song into the app.
  - While it downloads, a ring fills. Done, it turns into a filled circle with an arrow.
  - Pressing it then opens a small popover attached to it: "Remove from downloads?" with Keep/Remove. A download in progress can be cancelled the same way; a failed one shows why, with Try again.
- **Library → Audio → Downloaded:** a chip and a tile in All, listing every downloaded song whether or not it's saved anywhere.
  - Play, shuffle, sort and filter like Favorites; songs play from their files.
  - Removing rows deletes their downloads. "Manage downloads" opens Downloads → Music.
- **Save as MP3:**
  - The save sheet and Settings → Downloads offer MP3, encoded with LAME at the Downloads audio quality (Best = 320 kbps CBR). An MP3 download is copied as it is.
  - The sheet's Where section has "Choose a folder" (the system picker). The folder becomes Settings → Downloads' folder too, and is selected at once.

## Decisions
- **Ranking:**
  - `raygun-resolver::rank_for` keeps the match score in charge: only candidates within 6 points of the best (not counting the +50 for having played before) are reordered by bitrate distance.
  - The distance is the ratio between bitrate and target (64 vs 128 weighs like 256 vs 128). Unknown bitrates count as 160 kbps. Best prefers the highest.
- **Remembered picks:**
  - `local_stream_candidate.bitrate_kbps` (migration 0022) remembers what played.
  - A cached pick fits when within 60 % of a kbps target, or ≥ 256 kbps for Best; unknown bitrates always fit, so old rows still play at once.
  - The 10-minute candidate memo is keyed by track and target.
- **Mobile data:**
  - `raygun-app::network::on_mobile_data` asks the `raygun-audio` plugin (`networkKind`: ConnectivityManager transports; Wi-Fi or Ethernet wins over cellular). Needs `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, a normal permission.
  - The answer is reused for 10 s. Desktop is never on mobile data.
- **Scope:**
  - Sources that play their own stream directly (a track found on Internet Archive, the YouTube embed) aren't re-picked. YouTube's embed has no quality control the app can set.
  - Quality applies wherever the resolver chooses: lookups, fallbacks and downloads.
- **LAME:**
  - `mp3lame-encoder` 0.2.5 over a vendored `third_party/mp3lame-sys`. Upstream's build script picked its config by host OS, so a Windows machine building for Android used MSVC's `configMS.h`, whose `#define int8_t signed char` breaks Android's `<stdint.h>`.
  - The vendored copy builds the encoder with `cc` for every target from one `raygun_config.h`. Licences: LGPL-3.0 bindings and LGPL-2.0 LAME, compatible with AGPL.
  - The VBR/Info frame is off (CBR). Mono stays mono; more channels keep the front pair.
- The download button shows wherever + shows except the phone's player bar, which keeps its three controls.
- `Collection::Downloaded` (with `downloaded_songs`) makes the view a normal collection, so playing passes the shown order like other tables. `song_download_for` finds a song's download by its track key.
