# M46.25 — Search pills

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):** "in search, merge music and podcasts together, and add new default bubble option for Relevant and it show all songs artists albums or podcasts, also add podcasts pill option. for movies and tv series also put relevant and movies and series as pill options".

## Goal
- **Music and podcasts are one search type, Music.** Its pills are **Relevant** (the default), Songs, Artists, Albums and **Podcasts**.
  - Relevant shows a bit of everything, best first:
    - the top result: the best song by title score, or an artist or podcast whose name matches better;
    - five songs, an artists row, an albums row and a podcasts row.
  - Each part has "See all", which switches to its pill. Parts with nothing found are left out.
  - Podcasts shows what the old Podcasts type showed: the directory's matches, or top shows before anything is typed.
- **Old links still work:** `type=podcast` opens Music with the Podcasts pill. The All view's podcast row leads there too.
- **Movies & series** get pills **Relevant** (default), **Movies** and **Series**.
  - Relevant is today's view: the addons' rows, then the video sources' results.
  - Movies and Series keep only the addons' rows of that kind. Video sources don't say what a search result is until it's opened, so their results stay under Relevant, and the other two pills say so in one line.
- **The pill is part of the address** (`view=`), so Back returns to it.

## Decisions
- **Relevant isn't a new ranking service.** It reuses the searches each pill already makes (songs, artists, albums, podcasts), cached by React Query, so switching pills costs nothing.
- **The top result** is the song, artist or podcast whose name scores best against the query with `titleScore` (M46.11). On a tie, songs win.
- **Podcasts as a pill, not a type:** fewer types make the type row fit on phones, and the user expects podcasts beside music, as in Spotify.

## Verification
- **Tests:**
  - Relevant first: the top result (an artist named like the query), Albums and Podcasts parts, and See all moving to Songs;
  - old `type=podcast` links open the Podcasts pill;
  - Movies & series: Series leaves only series rows, with the note about sources.
  - The song-list tests now open the Songs pill.
- **Windows (release build):**
  - Search's types no longer have Podcasts.
  - Music → Relevant for "daft punk" showed a top result, five songs, ten albums and four podcasts, and Artists listed MusicBrainz's.
  - The top result was a song titled "Daft Punk" by another artist. An artist named like the query now wins when several of the songs found are theirs, taken from the artist search or from those songs.
- **Decision added:** the top result prefers an artist whose name is the query when two or more of the songs are theirs. Otherwise the best song by title score wins, then an artist, then a podcast.
