# M46.32 — Spoken word: authors, captions and transcription

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):** "audiobooks authors shouldnt show a music author page, it shows lyrics in the side panel when it should show captions/subtitles of audiobooks, and if no source for audiobooks then resize down and have button to setup ai to listen to audios in batches and show as text, local ai models as well".

## Goal
- **Raygun knows a spoken item:**
  - `spoken_item(source)` (`PodcastService::spoken_item`) says whether a queue item is a podcast episode or a book's chapter, and whose. It finds the episode by its audio address or its download, as progress-saving already did.
  - The UI asks once per item (`player/spoken.ts`, `useSpokenItem`). Queue items stay as they were.
- **Authors and shows aren't music artists:**
  - For a book chapter, the author links to the book's page. For a podcast episode, the show's name links to the show (`player/SpokenByline.tsx`). This applies in the side panel, the full player and the player bar.
  - About the artist and Credits are hidden, and so are the heart, Add to playlist, song tags and the song menu. The bar's right-click menu offers "Go to the book" or "Go to the show" instead.
- **Captions instead of lyrics** (`player/CaptionsView.tsx`), in the side panel's card, the full player's text view and the phone's cards:
  - **From the feed:** Podcasting 2.0 `<podcast:transcript>` in WebVTT, SRT or JSON (the timed formats, best first), stored as `<type>|<url>` in `podcast_episode.remote_transcript`. Fetched on first view and parsed by `captions/cues.rs`.
  - **From AI transcription**, when there's no transcript (below).
  - Lines follow playback like synced lyrics, and a tap seeks.
  - **With neither, the card is slim:** "No captions for this chapter" with **Set up transcription** (to Settings → AI, `#transcription`), or **Transcribe** once it's set up.
- **Transcription** (Settings → AI → Transcription, `Settings::transcription`, `TranscriptionPrefs`):
  - **On this device:** Whisper tiny (multilingual, 151 MB) from the AI catalogue, pinned by SHA-256 to a fixed Hugging Face revision. It runs in Rust with candle on the CPU (`raygun_ai::speech::Whisper`).
  - **Online:** an OpenAI-compatible provider from the translators list (OpenAI, Groq, a local server) and a model (`whisper-1` by default). It calls `POST {base}/audio/transcriptions` with `verbose_json` segments. The key stays in the encrypted secrets store.
  - Episodes are transcribed **in batches** (`raygun-app/src/captions/`, `CaptionService`):
    - 30-second windows from where the listener is, ahead of playback, then the part before it;
    - captions appear as each window finishes (`CaptionsEvent`);
    - results are kept per episode (`local_episode_caption`, with covered stretches in `local_caption_progress`), so nothing is transcribed twice;
    - one episode at a time;
    - after the listener transcribes a chapter, the next chapters of that book or show are transcribed when they come up.

## Decisions
- **candle, not whisper.cpp:** pure Rust builds for Android from Windows like the rest.
  - The tokenizer only decodes, from `tokenizer.json` (byte-level BPE), so the HF `tokenizers` crate isn't used directly.
  - Mel filters are computed (librosa's Slaney filters), not shipped.
- **Decoding:**
  - greedy, with timestamps and OpenAI's timestamp rules;
  - the language is detected from the first window and kept for the episode;
  - a sentence cut at a window's end is heard again whole in the next window (`Heard::consumed_ms`).
- **Silence and scraps aren't sent:** windows below an RMS of 0.002 or shorter than a second count as heard with no words. Whisper makes words up for those ("you").
- **Audio:**
  - from the downloaded file when there is one, else fetched once into `cache/captions/` and removed when the episode is done;
  - Symphonia decodes it (`captions/audio.rs`, seeking for jumps over 5 s);
  - mixed to mono and averaged down to 16 kHz.
- **Cost:** online transcription asks once before its first batch (`online_confirmed`), and never runs more than 10 minutes ahead of the listener.
- **Memory:** the model (about 150 MB) loads for a job and is let go when no job runs.
- Speed: a 30-second window takes about 1.8 s on a desktop CPU in a release build.

## Verification
- **Checks:** as M46.29.
  - Rust: cue parsing (`captions/cues.rs`), audio windows and downsampling (`captions/audio.rs`), silence, covered stretches (`raygun-db::captions`), mel filters, tokenizer decoding and the online answer (`raygun-ai::speech`).
  - UI: `player/Captions.test.tsx` (byline, captions card, slim state, Transcribe and Stop, settings).
  - Live check `whisper_live` on a 47 s spoken WAV (Windows text-to-speech):
    - every sentence came back word for word, with timings;
    - the language was detected as English;
    - about 1.8 s per 30 s window in a release build;
    - it once made up "you" for a near-silent last scrap, so quiet and sub-second windows are now skipped.
- **Windows (LibriVox "Poems by a Little Girl"):**
  - The side panel showed the author "Hilda Conkling" (to the book's page), the slim card "No captions for this chapter. · Set up transcription", and no artist or credits cards.
  - Set up transcription opened Settings → AI → Transcription. "On this device" showed the Whisper model, which downloaded (146 MB) and passed its hash check.
  - Transcribe: captions appeared within seconds and followed playback with the current line bright ("…Read by shrimpfish. Poems by a little girl by Hilda Conkling…"). The full player's Captions tab listed the whole chapter, "Transcribed on this device".
- **Android (LibriVox "The Art of War", debug build with the math crates optimized):**
  - The full player showed "Sun Tzu 孙武" and the slim Captions card, with no heart or add-to-playlist button.
  - The model downloaded in the emulator, and Transcribe filled the card while it played ("Hold out baits to entice the enemy…").
- **Found and fixed while checking:**
  - The card didn't notice transcription being set up until the chapter changed. It now refreshes on setting changes and finished model downloads, keeping the same episode's lines meanwhile.
  - Captions were drawn at 80 % of the lyrics size; now the same.
  - The phone bar and the desktop bar still offered the heart for chapters; hidden now.
- Test data removed afterwards on the emulator: the model, the setting (Off) and the test playlist. On Windows the model and setting were left as they are, since the app was being used with them.
