# M46.55 Answers in Search

Questions and calculations typed in Search get answers, as Wolfram|Alpha gives them: computed on
the device where they can be, looked up in open data where they can't, each with its sources.

## Where they show

- Search → All: one answer card above the results when the words plainly ask something (a
  calculation, a conversion, a date or time question, a fact, "what is …", "define …",
  "translate … to …"). It's asked beside the sources and shows when ready; any other search
  (`Blue`, `1984`, `8 Mile`, `9/11`) shows nothing and costs nothing (Rust reads the words at once).
- Search → Answers (a pill after Your library): every engine's answer in full: input
  interpretation, result (formulas as MathML), a units toggle, other forms and facts, summary and
  picture, sources with licences, related questions. Plain words get what they name (a summary
  and key facts); before anything is typed, examples.
- Settings → Browse → Answers: answers in All (on), look up facts online (on).

## Engines (`raygun-app/src/answers/`)

- On the device, offline: dates and times (`dates`: today, days until/since/between, dates some
  days away, weekdays, holidays, week numbers, leap years, ages, Unix time; `zones`: places,
  countries, abbreviations and offsets through chrono-tz), statistics (`stats`), algebra
  (`algebra`: exact fractions; linear and quadratic equations solved exactly with surds and
  complex roots, higher degrees numerically; expand, factor, derivative, integral), named
  constants (`constants`, CODATA 2018), percentages (`percent`), and fend (fend-core, MIT) for
  arithmetic, units, number bases, Roman numerals and words (`calc`, with `expr` reading
  arithmetic into TeX and telling calculations from titles, `units` naming units and toggles).
- Currencies: fend at the European Central Bank's euro reference rates (`currency`), fetched at
  most every 4 hours, kept for offline use, shown with their date and the ECB credited.
- Online, read from the words (`questions`): Wikidata facts (`wikidata`: `wbsearchentities`,
  `wbgetclaims` trying the first three things found until one has the property, `wbgetentities`
  for names in the app's language; CC0), Wikipedia summaries (`wikipedia`: the REST summary of the
  thing's article in the app's language, else English; CC BY-SA 4.0), definitions from the
  dictionaries (`words`, `DictionaryService`), translations by this device's models or Settings →
  AI's translators (none downloaded from here). Requests use the dictionaries' polite client
  (User-Agent naming Raygun, paced, `Retry-After` kept). Answers are kept a week (a miss a day)
  in `cache/answers/` and used offline; nothing is written while incognito; Settings → Data →
  Caches counts and clears them.
- Extensions: the `answers` provider kind (`answer({ query, language, card })` →
  `{ answers: [...] }`, read leniently by `raygun-ext-host::answers`, TeX made MathML by the
  host). Asked on the pill always, in All only for a plain question nothing else answered; never
  kept. SDK types, template (Open-Meteo weather) and guide page `docs/extensions/answers.md`.
  First-party `extensions/wolfram-alpha` (the person's own AppID as a required setting, every
  answer linked to its wolframalpha.com page).

## Reading the words

Questions are read in English. A query is a question only when the words say so: an operation
between numbers (not a lone `a/b` of two numbers up to 31 or a range of years or a code like
`1-800-273-8255`), a conversion with `to`/`in`, a date or time phrase, a question form, or a fact
named the way titles aren't (`population of`, but not `Children of Men`). Numbers or quantities
alone (`1984`, `8 Mile`) are answered on the pill only.
