# M42.1 — Navigation and screens modelled on the reference apps

**Relevant docs:**
- the reference apps' own screens: Mihon's store screenshots (`reference/mihon/fastlane/.../phoneScreenshots`), Aniyomi's `HomeScreen.kt` tabs, CloudStream's `fragment_home_head.xml` and `fragment_result*.xml`;
- M38–M42 (Watch, Library, Search, video sources).

## Why
A review of the app found:
- Watch is a whole tab when it belongs in the library.
- Forms (a folder path, "Open a stream or file", "Play a video link", an addon address) sit at the end of lists.
- Screens have no top bar, actions or back buttons.
- The look doesn't match any of the apps Raygun is built from.

All of them (Mihon, Aniyomi, CloudStream, Shosetsu) share one shape:
- a top app bar with the screen's title and icon actions (search, filter, overflow), and a back arrow on inner screens;
- tabs for sub-views;
- covers in a grid with the title over a gradient;
- list rows with a thumbnail and trailing actions;
- a floating "Resume" button on title screens;
- inputs only in the top bar or in dialogs and sheets.

## Design
- **Navigation** (bottom bar on phones, rail on wide screens): **Home · Library · Browse · Search · More**.
  - **Watch is no longer a tab.** Its Continue watching moves to Home and Library → Videos, its catalogues to Browse, and "Play a link" to an action. `/watch` now redirects to Library → Videos.
  - **The bar** is Material 3 style (Mihon): an icon with a pill behind it when active, and a label.
  - **The mini player** shows only while something is queued.
- **`TopBar`** (new, on every screen; replaces `PageHeader`):
  - a large title on tab screens, a back arrow and a smaller title on inner screens;
  - icon actions on the right, with an overflow menu (`OverflowMenu`);
  - it stays at the top while the page scrolls.
- **Home:**
  - Continue watching (video progress), Continue reading (manga and novel titles you read, newest first) and Recently played (music);
  - when everything is empty, one short prompt pointing to Browse and Library.
  - The "Welcome to Raygun" banner goes.
- **Library:**
  - **Top bar:** Library, with search (filters what's shown), filter (manga and novels: the existing filters) and an overflow menu: Open link or file, Add music folder, Find music on this device, Categories.
  - **Media tabs:** Music, Podcasts, Manga, Novels, Videos in one scrollable underline tab row, not a boxed switch plus chips. Music's own views (Albums, Artists, Tracks, Playlists, Favorites) stay as chips under Music only.
  - **Videos:** Continue watching first, then everything watched.
- **Browse** (Aniyomi's Browse), tabs **Sources · Extensions**:
  - **Sources:** a list grouped by kind (Movies & series, Manga, Novels). Stremio addons with catalogues count as Movies & series sources and open the addon's catalogue rows (the old Watch home). Each row has the source's initial, its name, the extension or addon, and a "Latest" button where the source has one.
  - **Top bar overflow:** Stremio addons and Extension repositories.
  - **Extensions:** the existing Installed, Browse and Repositories tabs.
- **More** (Mihon's More), one list of rows with icons: Updates, Downloads, Categories, Stremio addons, Extensions, Devices, AI, then Settings and About.
  - Settings keeps its cards on its own screen, reached from More.
- **Forms move out of lists:**
  - **Open link (sheet):** the music library's "Open a stream or file" and Watch's "Play a video link" become one Open link sheet, reached from the Library overflow.
  - **Add folder (dialog):** the folder path field becomes a dialog, from the Library overflow and the empty state's second button.
  - **"+" in the top bar:** adding an addon by address (Stremio addons), a repository (Extension repositories) and a playlist (Music → Playlists) each move to a "+" action in the top bar that opens a dialog.
- **Title screens** (video sources, Stremio, manga, novels):
  - **Header:** a blurred copy of the cover or backdrop behind the cover, title and facts (CloudStream and Mihon);
  - **Top bar:** back, refresh and an overflow menu (open in browser);
  - **Resume button:** a floating extended Play or Resume button at the bottom right, from Mihon's "Start" and "Resume".

## Out of scope
- New features: the video library (M43) stays as planned.
- A full design-system rewrite of every inner page: inner pages get the top bar and lose inline forms, and keep their content.

## Settings (added during the milestone)
- **Shape (Mihon's):** a list of categories, each with a line saying what's inside, and one screen per category built from preference rows.
- **Categories:**
  - **Appearance:** theme (with previews), accent colour, language, start screen, navigation bar labels.
  - **Library:** covers per row, title placement on covers, unread and downloaded badges, the manga update interval, categories, music folders.
  - **Reader:** novel text size, line spacing, typeface, text width, turning pages, reading-aloud speed.
  - **Player:** preferred quality, resume, watched point, double-tap skip, automatic subtitles, subtitle language.
  - **Browse:** NSFW, extensions, repositories, Stremio addons.
  - **Data and backup.**
  - **Connections:** devices, LAN remote, AI, MCP.
- **Every option does something.** New fields on the Rust `Settings`, each validated:
  - **UI options:** `accent`, `startScreen`, `navLabels`, `gridColumns`, `coverStyle`, `unreadBadges`, `downloadBadges`.
  - **Player options:** `resumeVideos`, `watchedPercent`, `seekSeconds`, `autoSubtitles`, `subtitleLanguage`. `video.rs` reads them when a video opens and when the player reports:
    - resume off starts at 0;
    - the watched point replaces the fixed 90% (and the 95% resume cut-off);
    - the seek step and auto-subtitle language go to `VideoPlayerActivity`, which sets ExoPlayer's seek increments and preferred text language.
  - **Stremio:** subtitle ordering follows the subtitle language.

## Decisions
- **Five tabs (Home, Library, Browse, Search, More)**, Aniyomi's and Mihon's set, with Search kept as a tab since Raygun searches across media.
  - **Updates and History** are rows in More, not tabs: Raygun has music, podcasts and video besides manga, so they're one kind among several.
- **Watch's pieces moved; nothing was dropped:**
  - Continue watching is on Home and Library → Videos;
  - Stremio catalogues are Browse sources;
  - video sources are Browse sources with Latest;
  - Play a link is "Open link or file" in the Library menu. It joined the music library's "Open a stream or file" as one sheet with a Video / Audio switch.
- **Old addresses redirect:** `/watch` → Library → Videos, `/extensions` → Browse → Extensions (or the repositories screen), `/manga/browse` and `/novel/browse` → Browse.
- **No inline forms remain.** Each moved into a `Sheet`:
  - folder path, repository, addon address, category name, playlist name and podcast feed (each from a "+" or menu item);
  - the library search, which is the top bar's search mode;
  - the manga and novel filters, which open from the filter icon.
- **The mini player** shows only while something is queued.
- **Compact covers by default** (titles over a gradient), as in Mihon. Comfortable puts them underneath.
- **Locale clean-up:**
  - keys English no longer has were removed from every locale (137 strings);
  - translations of five strings whose English meaning changed were dropped too, so they show correct English until retranslated: Open link or file, Add repository, New category, Add by feed address, Browse sources.
- **Not done:**
  - **manga reader settings** (reading direction is per title today);
  - **a downloads category** (no download options exist yet);
  - **a settings search.**

## Second pass (polish)
- **Top bar:** it sat about 25 px low. The sticky offset repeated the status-bar inset that the scroller's padding already applies, so it's now `top-0`. Tabs attach directly under the bar.
- **Search:** the field is the top bar itself (a rounded search bar, as in CloudStream and Aniyomi), not a title over a separate box. A source that's still searching says so instead of leaving a gap.
- **Chips:** selected chips are tonal everywhere (Material 3 filter chips, as in Mihon), never a solid block: search types, seasons, genres, filters, extension kinds and the source lists.
- **Player:**
  - the mini player is a floating rounded card above the navigation bar;
  - the full player has the artwork blurred behind it, the title and artist on the left with the heart and add-to-playlist on the right, a larger rounded play button, and Up next with thumbnails and artists (RiPlay's layout).
- **Extensions:**
  - rows are flat with a letter icon and a BUILT-IN badge, not in a grey box;
  - Reload and Refresh are top-bar actions on Browse;
  - the detail page has a header with a large icon, then Provides (capabilities named in words, e.g. "Song search") and Network access;
  - settings without a label read as words (`itemsPerSearch` → "Items per search"), done in the extension host for every extension.
- **Empty states:** a shared `shell/EmptyState` (large tonal icon, title, line, action) for Downloads, Updates, Favorites, Library → Manga, Novels and Videos. The downloads count hides at zero.
- **Readers:**
  - the manga reader has Mihon's controls: a solid top bar with title and chapter, a reading-mode button opening a sheet (the select is gone), and at the bottom the page slider in a pill between round previous and next chapter buttons;
  - the novel reader matches it.
- **Manga title actions:** Download shows a count badge instead of a two-line label.

## Third pass: Search moves into Browse
Two tabs for finding things made no sense, so Search joins Browse (as CloudStream and Mihon do: a search bar over the sources). The Library keeps only what you have (6d0e200).
- **Bottom bar:** Home · Library · Browse · More.
- **Browse:**
  - The header is the search bar, with the overflow menu (Reload or Refresh on Extensions, Addons, Repositories).
  - Under it are three tabs: **Search** · **Sources** · **Extensions**. Sources stays the default.
  - Typing switches to Search. The query and kind live in the URL (`/browse?tab=search&type=…&q=…`), so Back returns to the same results.
  - The Search tab is the old Search page (`search/SearchPanel.tsx`): kind chips, everything-at-once results, per-kind sources with filters, and top podcast shows before anything is typed.
  - The selected kind's chip scrolls into view.
- **Links:**
  - `/search` redirects to Browse → Search, keeping the query and kind.
  - The Library's empty states ("Find in Browse"), Home's search action and Get started open Browse → Search on the right kind.
  - The manga and novel empty states no longer repeat their title in the line under it.
- **Settings:** the start screen is still Home, Library or Browse.
