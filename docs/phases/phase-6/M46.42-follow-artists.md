# M46.42 — Follow artists, with new releases and concerts

## Goal
- **Follow** on artist pages (and in artist menus): the artist goes into Library → Audio → Artists (followed first, with a mark) and quick access (M46.41). Unfollow takes it out of both.
- **A bell beside Follow** turns on alerts for that artist:
  - **New releases:** every day, followed artists with alerts are checked through `artistReleases` (M46.36: Deezer, MusicBrainz). A release newer than the last check is announced once (`ArtistReleaseEvent`, a system notification on Android and desktop) and listed in Updates → Releases.
  - **Concerts:** MusicBrainz events (open data: `/ws/2/event?artist=<mbid>`) in the coming months are listed on the artist page and in Updates → Releases. There's no free, keyless concert feed beyond it (Bandsintown and Songkick need registered keys), so coverage is what MusicBrainz has.
- **Storage:** `raygun-db::followed_artists` (synced: name, provider ids, alerts, followed at; tombstones), `local_artist_release_seen` (device-local).
- **Live check:** `follow_live` (a followed artist's latest release and events).

## Decisions
- **No RSS:** the music services Raygun reads have no per-artist feeds; their release lists are the same data, checked daily in the background (`ArtistAlerts` in `raygun-app`).

## Verification

- Live: `follow_live_artist_events` found coming events from MusicBrainz.
- Test: `only_unseen_releases_of_this_year_are_news`.
