# M46.22 — Audiobooks

**Asked by the user (2026-11-01):** "i want you to also add Audio Books as a new media type", with its own pill in Library → Audio. Sources: LibriVox and the Internet Archive (research, 2026-11-02, in the scratchpad's `audiobook_sources.md`).

## Goal
- **A kind of its own:**
  - Library → Audio gets an **Audiobooks** chip: books you've added, each with its cover, author, and how far you are ("Chapter 4 of 37", a bar).
  - Browse all gets an **Audiobooks** tile leading to `/audiobooks`: search, plus Popular and Newest rows in carousels with arrow buttons on wide windows.
- **A book's page** (`/audiobook?id=`):
  - Cover, title, author, total length and description.
  - **Resume** (the first chapter not finished, at its position) or **Play from the start**, and **Add to library**.
  - Chapters in order, each with its length and progress, a played mark, a download and the podcast right-click menu.
- **Playing:**
  - Playing a chapter queues the rest of the book after it, so it plays on.
  - Each chapter remembers its position (as podcast episodes do), and Raygun reopens on it paused after a restart (M46.16).
- **Sources:**
  - A new provider kind `audiobook.directory` (`search`, `popular`, `latest`, `subject`, paged).
  - First-party **LibriVox**: the Internet Archive's `librivoxaudio` collection for free-text search and popularity; each book's LibriVox project number gives its chapter feed (`librivox.org/rss/<number>`).

## Decisions
- **A book is a feed:**
  - LibriVox publishes a feed per book, so a book is stored as a podcast row with `remote_kind = 'audiobook'` (migration 0023). It reuses refresh, progress, downloads, the stream proxy and device sync's columns.
  - Podcast lists leave audiobooks out, and the audiobook list holds only them.
- **Chapter order:**
  - LibriVox feeds have no dates; `itunes:type` is `serial` and each item has an `itunes:episode` number.
  - The feed parser now reads the number (`remote_number`, migration 0023), falling back to the item's position in a serial feed.
  - Books list chapters by number; podcasts keep newest first.
- **Titles:**
  - LibriVox titles its feeds "Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen (1775 - 1817)", with the author "LibriVox".
  - When a book has no author or LibriVox's, its title splits at the last " by ", and the dates are dropped. This happens on refresh, and when listing books stored before.
  - Chapters play under the book's author, with the book as the album.
- **Covers:** each book uses the Internet Archive's thumbnail. LibriVox's newest uploads often have no cover yet and show the Archive's waveform picture until they get one.
- **Opening a book:** `audiobook_open(feedUrl)` opens a feed as a book: stored on first sight, not added to the library until Add.

## Verification
- **Tests:**
  - a serial feed numbering its chapters;
  - books keeping their chapter order and staying out of podcast lists;
  - LibriVox titles splitting into the book and its author, and real authors kept;
  - UI: books in Library → Audio with how far each is, the empty state, Resume and Add to library, finding and opening a book.
  - All app tests (432), clippy, `cargo test --workspace`.
- **Live check (`librivox_live`):** each list's first book came with its feed. Search found Pride and Prejudice (Jane Austen). Popular began with The Art of War and Newest with The Radio Boys at Ocean Point.
- **Android (debug build):**
  - Browse all → Audiobooks listed Popular and Newest.
  - Alice's Adventures in Wonderland opened with 12 chapters (2 h 44 min). Play started Chapter 1, paused after a few seconds.
  - Add to library put it in Audio → Audiobooks ("Chapter 1 of 12") and in Audio → All's new Audiobooks row.
  - Its page showed the feed's own title, "… by Lewis Carroll (1832 - 1898)", with the author LibriVox. That led to the title decision above; the final build shows "Alice's Adventures in Wonderland" by Lewis Carroll. The test book was removed from the library afterwards.
- **Windows (release build):**
  - Audio → Audiobooks showed the empty state. Find audiobooks opened the browse page: a loader on Popular, then its covers, and Newest.
  - Pride and Prejudice opened as "Pride and Prejudice" by Jane Austen, 37 chapters, 13 h 7 min. Play started "Chapters 1-3" (18:52).
  - The player bar named the book as the artist, split at "and" ("Pride, Prejudice"). Chapters now play under the author; the final build showed Jane Austen.
