# M46.33 — Video player tools: panels, subtitles, AI subtitles, volume, players, keys

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02), in one batch with M46.34–M46.38:**
- "the video panels do not need to be this big, have a good popup attached size and have scrollability, subtitles make sure it looks for sources, and have option for ai subtitles, and be able to make subtitles as you are watching or offline with right click menu, requires downloaded model and choose which"
- "subtitle delay, make it so you can type specific amount"
- "allow audio level to be higher than 100% up to 200% past the original audio"
- "the play in external source button should let you pick which player, not one click"
- "make sure you have video player keybinds and a button to see them"

## Goal
- **Panels are popups**, not full-height side sheets. Episodes, Sources, Speed, Subtitles, Audio, Quality, Aspect and Players open above the control bar on the right. They're as tall as their content up to about 60 % of the player, and scroll.
- **Subtitles look for sources:**
  - **Find subtitles** asks every installed addon that serves subtitles for the title, by its Stremio id.
  - When none does, it offers to add OpenSubtitles v3 (Stremio's own subtitles addon) in one tap.
  - Found subtitles join the list with their language.
- **AI subtitles** (Whisper on this device, or the online provider from Settings → AI):
  - **While watching:** "Make subtitles with AI" in the Subtitles panel. You choose the speech model from the installed ones; with none, it links to download one. It transcribes the video's audio ahead of where you are, a window at a time, and the lines appear as a track named "AI subtitles". Stop is in the same place.
  - **Offline:** a downloaded video's menu (right-click or long-press) has "Make subtitles". It transcribes the whole file in the background.
  - Kept per video, in the captions tables (key: the video's progress key). Both players get them as a WebVTT track (Android too).
  - The audio comes from:
    - a downloaded file or a progressive stream (symphonia; MP4, Matroska/WebM, MP3, AAC), reading streams with HTTP ranges;
    - HLS, from its MPEG-TS or fMP4 segments, with a small TS demuxer passing ADTS AAC to symphonia.
  - A second speech model, Whisper base (multilingual, about 290 MB, pinned by SHA-256), joins the catalogue, so there's a choice.
- **Subtitle delay:** the delay is a number field. Type an exact value in seconds (−60 to 60, to 0.01 s), or nudge with −0.1/+0.1.
- **Volume up to 200 %:** the slider and keys go to 200 % on desktop (a gain node past 100 %). On Android the boost setting now defaults to 100 %, so 200 %.
- **Other players:** the external-player button opens a list of installed players (mpv, VLC, MPC-HC, MPC-BE, PotPlayer), and Raygun remembers the last one picked. "Always use another app" uses that one.
- **Keys:** the desktop player handles:
  - Space/K play, ←/→ seek, J/L ±10 s, ↑/↓ volume, M mute, F fullscreen;
  - C subtitles, [ ] speed, N/P next/previous episode, , . frame step when paused, 0–9 jump to that tenth, Esc close panel or player, ? shortcuts.
  - A keyboard button in the top bar shows the list.

## Decisions
- **One captions pipeline:** video subtitles reuse M46.32's `CaptionService` with a different audio source (`VideoAudio`), so batching, kept stretches, models and online cost rules are shared.
- **The track is a file:** the cues are written to `cache/captions/<key>.vtt` and served through the proxy's `/local/` route. Both players take it like any external subtitle, so the Kotlin player needs no new code.
- **OpenSubtitles is offered, never added silently:** it's a third-party addon, so it's the user's tap that installs it.

## Verification
- Checks: `pnpm lint`, `pnpm type-check`, `pnpm test` (456), `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace` all pass.
- Live: `video_whisper_live` heard Tears of Steel's dialogue through the HLS reader (AES-128 segments) with Whisper; the external-player arguments are unit-tested for every player.
- Windows (release build): playing a film with no stream showed the Sources panel as a compact popover beside its button. The keys sheet needs a loaded video and is covered by `VideoPlayer.test.tsx` (the `?` key and the button).
