# M46.7 — Customization: colour, cover sizes, reader zoom, the player and its panel

**Relevant docs:** M42.1 (Settings rows, accents), M46 (library layouts and columns), M27 (the manga reader), M46.5 (layout), M46.6 (readers on wide windows).

## Goal
- **Any accent colour**, beside the seven presets.
- **Cover sizes:**
  - smaller covers by default on wide windows, in Browse, Search, source pages and the Library (manga, novels, videos);
  - a cover-size setting for when covers per row is automatic.
- **Zoom in the manga reader:**
  - how a page fits in the paged modes (screen, width, height, original size);
  - how wide the webtoon column is;
  - both settable in the viewer itself and in Settings → Reader.
- **Rows say what they are:** a Stremio addon's catalogs in Search show their names ("Popular · Movies", "New · Series"), as Watch's rows do, instead of repeating "Movies".
- **The reader's page bar:** narrower on wide windows (70 %), its width and buttons (chapters, zoom, reading mode, full screen) set in Display; pages to load ahead (1–10).
- **Everywhere:** F11 full screen; hover labels on icon buttons; scrollbars (thin over a clear track, wide, hidden) and animations (lively, calm, off) in Settings → Appearance.
- **Music:**
  - long titles scroll (bar, panel, full player);
  - a Now playing side panel on wide windows (artwork or video, title, lyrics, next in queue; the queue as a tab), beside the page instead of over it;
  - the full player wider on desktop, side by side (artwork and controls left, lyrics or Up next right) or stacked, with its width, lyrics size and alignment and background in Settings → Player and its own Layout button;
  - Up next as a view of its own, so the controls never move;
  - a playhead that glides and follows the audio;
  - YouTube search without a key.
- **Menus:** on wide windows, sheets opened from a button (choices in Settings, track actions, the player's layout, extension info) pop up beside it and close on a click elsewhere, Escape or the button again.
- **Extensions:** Install installs at once; the info button beside it shows what an extension may access; several can be selected and installed in one go, and all updates at once.

## Design
- **Settings (Rust `Settings`):**
  - `accent` gains `custom`, with `accent_color` (`#rrggbb`, checked);
  - `cover_width` (0 = automatic, else one of `COVER_WIDTHS` in px);
  - `manga_reader: MangaReaderPrefs { paged_scale: fitScreen | fitWidth | fitHeight | original, webtoon_width: 0 (automatic) or 30–100 % in steps of 5 }`, one `serde(default)` row.
- **Accent:**
  - `useApplyTheme` sets `--accent-on-dark` and `--accent-on-light` from the colour with CSS relative colour: the same hue and chroma, with the lightness kept in a readable range for each theme (`clamp`).
  - Settings → Appearance adds a colour swatch that opens the system colour picker.
- **Cover grids** (`manga/coverGrid.ts`, used by `MangaGrid` and `LibraryItems`):
  - a fixed number of columns when set;
  - otherwise `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(<size>, 1fr))`, where automatic is 6.5 rem on phones (three covers, as before) and 8 rem from 768 px.
  - The setting is in Settings → Library and in the library's Display sheet.
- **Reader:**
  - **Paged scale:**
    - fit screen is the default;
    - fit width scrolls down a page;
    - fit height scrolls across;
    - original size scrolls both ways.
    - Double-tap zoom still works.
  - **Webtoon width:**
    - automatic is the full width on phones, at most 768 px on wide windows;
    - a percentage sets the column against the window's width.
  - **Where:** a "Display" button in the reader's top bar opens a sheet with both; Settings → Reader has a Manga group.
- **Stremio rows:** Search's popular rows (nothing typed) are catalogs, so they show the catalog's name and See all. Search results still show the kind alone, since an addon's search catalog name says nothing.

## Decisions
- **YouTube without a key (extension 2.0.0):**
  - search goes through YouTube Music's own web API (InnerTube `youtubei/v1/search`, client `WEB_REMIX`) with its Songs and Videos filters: songs first (albums, linked artists), music videos to fill up;
  - no key, cookies or account; a refused client version is re-read from music.youtube.com's page and retried once;
  - a personal Data API key, if set, still switches to the official API;
  - playback stays in YouTube's embedded player. Taking the streams out of YouTube (signature deciphering, proof-of-origin tokens) would defeat its bot checks, so Raygun doesn't.
- **The playhead:**
  - YouTube's embed kept reporting the previous video's time and state for a moment after a new load, and those reports were taken as the new song's. A fallback stream could then start at the old song's time.
  - Every embed reply now carries the load it's about (`token`); the page only reports time once the new video has started or is cued, and checks its video id.
  - The bar, the full player and lyrics share `useLivePosition`, which moves the position between reports; sliders step in quarter seconds, and arrow keys still jump 5 s.
- **Side panel and full player:**
  - the side panel is part of the page's row (it narrows the page, Spotify's way) and remembers whether it was open (`localStorage`, a viewer convenience);
  - the one video dock follows whichever surface is showing: the full player over the panel;
  - the full player's views (`artwork`, `lyrics`, `queue`) replace the lyrics flag; side by side, the artwork stays on the left and the right column shows lyrics or Up next as tabs;
  - its layout is `Settings::player_view` (`PlayerViewPrefs`: layout auto/side by side/stacked, width 40–100 % or by screen, lyrics size 14–48 px or by screen, alignment, background).
- **Popovers:**
  - `shell/Popover` is portalled to the body and placed under or over its button, kept on screen and following scrolls;
  - popovers stack, so a choice inside one opens another above it, and Escape closes the newest only;
  - `Sheet` takes an `anchor` and becomes a popover on wide windows; phones keep bottom sheets.
- **Extension installs:**
  - one queue (`extensions/installQueue.ts`) installs one at a time, since each install reloads the extension host;
  - it keeps going when the screen is left, and failures stay on their rows with Try again;
  - the confirmation sheet is gone, and its contents are the info popover.
- **Scrollbars and animations:**
  - `Settings::scrollbars` and `Settings::motion` are set as `data-` attributes on the root;
  - scrollbars use WebView2's `::-webkit-scrollbar` (the standard properties would switch those off);
  - animations live in the base layer, so a component's own transitions win; the system's reduced motion turns them off, and Off also makes motion's springs instant.
- **Published packages:** a repacked package keeps its version's bytes. The packer rebuilt podcast-directory 1.0.0 with different bytes, so the published zip and its checksum were restored.

## Verification
- **Checks:**
  - Rust: settings accept and check the accent colour, cover widths, reader prefs, the player's layout, scrollbars and animations; the YouTube extension against a fake YouTube Music (a refused client version re-read from its page, songs then videos, region) and the key path; live: `cargo test -p raygun-ext-host -- --ignored youtube_live` finds "Get Lucky" with artists, album and length, without a key.
  - Vitest (333):
    - the custom accent; cover sizes; the reader's fit, zoom and page bar;
    - the side panel (art, lyrics, next up, queue), the full player side by side or stacked and its layout popover, Up next as a view;
    - popovers closing on a second click or a click elsewhere;
    - YouTube embed replies about an older load dropped;
    - one-click, selected and failed installs;
    - Search asking one source at a time, and only until a preview has two rows;
    - animations and scrollbars on the page.
  - Clippy, formatting, type-check and lint clean.
- 🖥️ **On Windows (release build):**
  - the new icon in the title bar, the taskbar and the side rail;
  - Search with nothing typed: rows filled in as they came into view; RoyalRoad, Rulate and ScribbleHub showed "HTTP 403" on their rows, and the app kept running. The previous build had crashed twice on this.
  - a song:
    - the Now playing panel beside the page with artwork, synced lyrics and next up;
    - the bar's playhead moving smoothly;
    - the full player side by side with Lyrics and Up next tabs and a thin scrollbar at the column's edge;
    - Add to playlist as a popover that closes on a second click and a click elsewhere;
    - a new song starting at 0:00; long titles scrolling.
- **The crash:**
  - The release profile had `panic = "abort"`. On Windows, Lua's errors (Shosetsu scripts through mlua) unwind as SEH exceptions through Rust frames, and with abort any script error, even an HTTP 403, ended the app ("panic in a function that cannot unwind").
  - Release builds now unwind; debug tests always did, which is why they passed.
  - Searches over many sources and addons go a few at a time: 4 sources, 6 addons, 4 repositories. Search's rows load as they come into view, 3 requests at a time.
- **Icons:**
  - from the `raygun_r_icon` set (`apps/raygun/src-tauri/icons/source/`);
  - desktop icons and the favicon use the transparent mark;
  - Android uses an adaptive icon: the transparent mark at 72 % over the logo's obsidian (`#0A0C14`), since adaptive icons need a background layer;
  - the playback notification's small icon is the mark as a white silhouette (`media3_notification_small_icon`);
  - the LAN remote's page has it inline.
  - The Android icons are still to be seen on the emulator (it stays off while desktop is tested).
