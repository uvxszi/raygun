# M46.14 — Rows that page, and the phone's full player

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):**
- "for the home and browse pages, dont do the scrollable lists horizontally, just have buttons on the side of the courasel you can press and it smoothly scrolls to the next side of the list, and you can also hold shift and scroll and it scrolls like before, still show the show all button for it, and remove the scrollbar that shows under it";
- "on mobile you have this scrollbar horizontal on tags, just hide the scrollbar itself but still allow scrolling";
- "in the music player on mobile, put the 'up next' button as a pill button a bit under the cover of the song, and i want you to move the player itself higher so the pause button and controls arent at the very bottom of the screen, also have a lyrics card below the player you can scroll into, and below that is the artist and below that is the credits, like spotify has those"; "the cards under the player are clickable to show more info and show full screen lyrics".

**Relevant docs:** M46.7 (full player), M46.8 (artist pages, rows and the wheel), M46.9 (discovery, MusicBrainz), the queued "Artist and song info" item in the roadmap.

## Goal
- **Rows** (`shell/Carousel.tsx`), wherever cards scroll sideways (Home, Browse, Library's rows, Watch rows):
  - buttons at the ends glide a page and show on hover;
  - Shift with the wheel scrolls the row, the plain wheel scrolls the page;
  - a finger still swipes it;
  - "Show all" stays, and no scrollbar shows.
- **Chips and tabs:** rows of chips, tabs and selection actions scroll with no visible scrollbar (`no-scrollbar`).
- **Phone's full player:**
  - the cover, an "Up next" pill under it, then the song and the controls, higher up the screen;
  - below it, cards to scroll into: Lyrics (opens the lyrics full screen), About the artist (picture, short bio; opens the artist page), Credits (performers, writers, producers; opens them all).
- **Artist info and credits** come from open data through a new provider kind `music.info` (MusicBrainz relationships, Wikipedia's summaries through Wikidata), cached in Rust.
