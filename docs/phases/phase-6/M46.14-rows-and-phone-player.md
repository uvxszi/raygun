# M46.14 — Rows that page, and the phone's full player

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):**
- "for the home and browse pages, dont do the scrollable lists horizontally, just have buttons on the side of the courasel you can press and it smoothly scrolls to the next side of the list, and you can also hold shift and scroll and it scrolls like before, still show the show all button for it, and remove the scrollbar that shows under it";
- "on mobile you have this scrollbar horizontal on tags, just hide the scrollbar itself but still allow scrolling";
- "in the music player on mobile, put the 'up next' button as a pill button a bit under the cover of the song, and i want you to move the player itself higher so the pause button and controls arent at the very bottom of the screen, also have a lyrics card below the player you can scroll into, and below that is the artist and below that is the credits, like spotify has those"; "the cards under the player are clickable to show more info and show full screen lyrics".

**Relevant docs:** M46.7 (full player), M46.8 (artist pages, rows and the wheel), M46.9 (discovery, MusicBrainz), the queued "Artist and song info" item in the roadmap.

## Goal
- **Rows** (`shell/Carousel.tsx`), wherever cards scroll sideways (Home, Browse, Library's rows, Watch rows):
  - buttons at the ends glide a page and show on hover;
  - Shift with the wheel scrolls the row, the plain wheel scrolls the page;
  - a finger still swipes it;
  - "Show all" stays, and no scrollbar shows.
- **Chips and tabs:** rows of chips, tabs and selection actions scroll with no visible scrollbar (`no-scrollbar`).
- **Phone's full player:**
  - the cover, an "Up next" pill under it, then the song and the controls, higher up the screen;
  - below it, cards to scroll into: Lyrics (opens the lyrics full screen), About the artist (picture, short bio; opens the artist page), Credits (performers, writers, producers; opens them all).
- **Artist info and credits** come from open data through a new provider kind `music.info` (MusicBrainz relationships, Wikipedia's summaries through Wikidata), cached in Rust.

## Decisions
- **Rows:**
  - `Carousel` wraps the row's `ul`, which keeps its own gap and padding. The buttons appear on hover and are hidden where there is no hover (`(hover:none)`), so phones swipe.
  - A page is 85 % of the row's width, so the last card of one page shows again at the start of the next. Which end is reached is worked out from the scroll position, on scroll and when a `ResizeObserver` sees the row or its cards change size.
  - `useHorizontalWheel` acts only with Shift held. The plain wheel scrolls the page, as Spotify's does.
- **`no-scrollbar`** is a Tailwind v4 `@utility` (`scrollbar-width: none` and `::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none }`). It applies to chip, tab and selection rows; the rows still scroll.
- **The phone's player** is the full player's stacked layout. It is used on phones and wherever Settings → Player picks Stacked; side by side is unchanged.
  - In the artwork view the body scrolls:
    - the cover, capped at `100dvh − 28rem` so the controls sit high and the first card peeks below them;
    - the "Up next" pill, with the next song's title, which opens the queue;
    - the controls;
    - the cards.
  - The lyrics and the queue open full screen above the controls, with the header's picture button leading back. The header's own lyrics and queue toggles are gone in this layout, because the cards and the pill replace them.
  - Stacked, the sheet drags only from its header (`useDragControls`, `touch-none` on the header), so the body scrolls freely.
- **The cards** (`player/PlayerCards.tsx`):
  - **Lyrics** shows five lines from the one playing (plain lyrics from the top) and opens full screen.
  - **About the artist** covers the credit's first artist: a picture, where they're from and their active years, and three lines of the bio. It opens `/artist`.
  - **Credits** lists the first three roles and opens a sheet with all of them. Each name links to its artist, and the song's page at the source opens in Raygun's browser (`music_open_info_page`). When no source knows the song, the queue item's artists are credited as performers.
- **Following a link from the player closes it** once the router's location changes (an effect in `PlayerSheet`). Closing first would pop the sheet's extra history entry and undo the navigation, which happened on Windows.
- **`music.info`** is a new provider kind:
  - `artist(name)` returns `{ artist }`; `credits(query)` returns `{ credits: [{role, names}], url }`.
  - The host asks providers in order and keeps the first that answers (`artist_info`, `song_credits`). Fields are trimmed and capped, and links must be http(s).
  - `ExtensionService` remembers answers for the run, including "nobody knew".
  - Pictures go through the cover cache (`ImageLinks::cover`).
- **MusicBrainz 1.3.0** implements `music.info`:
  - The artist comes from a search with an exact name match (else a score of 95 or more). Members come from "member of band" relations; origin and active years from the begin area and life span.
  - The bio and picture come from Wikipedia's summary of the page Wikidata links. The manifest gains `www.wikidata.org` and `en.wikipedia.org`.
  - Credits come from the recording's artist credit, its artist relations and its work's relations (composer, lyricist, writer). They are ordered Performed by, Written by, Composed by, Lyrics by, Produced by, then the rest.
- **The artist page** shows the same facts in an "About" section after Popular: kind, origin and years, the full bio, members, the source, and "Read more". Its picture prefers the artist's photo over album art.
- Not built from the queued "Artist and song info" item: similar artists, an online discography, and song facts such as samples and covers.
