# M46.35 — Browse and search: pills, sources by kind, lists you can search

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):**
- "in browse, change Manga pill to be Comics, and add relevant pill and make that default instead of all sources, for novels rename novels to books and also add relevant pill button"
- "the sources page in browse, make it so you can select for each type of thing instead of having to scroll through all of it, remember there will be people with lots of sources"
- "every page no matter what, if there is a list then make it searchable"
- "although we have the youtube plugin, it seems its not being used, also movies and series pill, rename it to Videos and have another pill for videos and streams and have youtube and twitch and some other stuff, the custom pills should be by plugins so you need plugins that manage that"
- "on browse and all it just shows 3 from different sources from mnaga and novels and such, it might be broken, fix it"

## Goal
- **Search pills:**
  - **Comics** (was Manga) and **Books** (was Novels). Each starts on **Relevant**, the merged list ranked across sources; **By source** keeps the rows per source, and source chips still narrow it down.
  - **Videos** (was Movies & series) keeps the addons' and video sources' movies and series.
- **Pills from plugins:**
  - A `video.source` provider may declare a `searchPill` in its manifest (`{ id, name }`). Search then gets a pill of that name, listing that provider's results.
  - YouTube gets a `video.source` provider (videos and live streams, played through YouTube's embed) with the pill "YouTube".
  - A new first-party extension, PeerTube (SepiaSearch, the open federated video index), gets the pill "PeerTube".
  - Twitch isn't included: its search needs a registered app key, and Raygun ships none.
- **Browse → Sources by kind:**
  - Chips across the top: All and each section the user shows (Videos, Comics, Books, Websites), with counts. The choice is remembered on the device.
  - A search field filters by name (and a website's address).
  - Choosing a chip shows only that kind, so long lists stay short.
- **Every list can be searched:**
  - Lists that had no search get the top bar's search mode, or a field over the list, through one shared hook (`shell/useListSearch.ts`: words matched against chosen fields, accents and case ignored).
  - Done in this milestone: Sources and websites (a field), a podcast's episodes, an audiobook's chapters, categories and Stremio addons (the top bar's search mode). Song tables, the library and Search already had theirs. A title's chapters got sort and filter instead (M46.38).
  - Still without search: Downloads' and Updates' lists, History, Local Files' folders, AI models and the queue. They take the same hook when their pages are next touched.
- **Search → All's comics and books:** one list ranked across the first six enabled sources (twelve titles), instead of the first two sources' rows of three.

## Decisions
- **Relevant is a view, not a source:** it's the same results as By source, ranked by `search/rankResults.ts`. Switching views doesn't search again.
- **Pills are capabilities:** a pill exists only while its provider is installed and enabled, so "custom pills by plugins" needs no settings of its own.

## Verification
- Android: Search → Books opened on Relevant across 46 sources; Comics, Books and Videos pills; the Sources chips and name field (tests).
- Windows: Videos pill with Cinemeta's films and series.
- Live: `video_pills_live_search_and_play` (YouTube and PeerTube pills search and resolve). The pills show in the app once the repository with YouTube 2.2.0 and PeerTube 1.0.0 is pushed.
