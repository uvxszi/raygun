## Goal
- **Song tables everywhere:**
  - Favorites, playlists, the library's songs, albums, artists, history and music search results share one table;
  - sorting by clicking a column, or from a Sort menu (Spotify's), and filtering inside the list;
  - two views: list (artwork, two lines) and compact (one line);
  - columns can be shown, hidden, dragged into another order and resized;
  - playing from a sorted list plays in that order.
- **Saving with a Plus:**
  - the heart on songs becomes a Plus (a check once saved);
  - pressing it saves to Favorites and opens Spotify's Add to playlist popover: search, New playlist made on the spot, Favorites ticked, every playlist with a tick, Cancel and Done.
- **Artists:**
  - artist names everywhere are links (each artist of "A, B & C" on its own);
  - the artist page shows popular songs from your sources, what's in your library (albums and songs) and your saved songs by them.
- **Panels you can resize:**
  - the side rail (down to covers only, as Spotify's library), the Now playing panel and the full player's split;
  - drag handles that also work with the keyboard, sizes remembered.
  - The rail lists Favorites and your playlists, as Spotify's library does.
- **The video in the side panel and the full player can be clicked** (YouTube's own play/pause, and "watch on YouTube").
- **Power-user extras:**
  - Play next, Add to queue, and dragging the queue into order; save the queue as a playlist;
  - several songs selected at once (Ctrl and Shift click, or a long press) for bulk actions: add to a playlist or the queue, remove, play;
  - a right-click menu on songs;
  - keyboard shortcuts for playback, plus the system's media controls and media keys on desktop (Media Session);
  - a sleep timer (minutes or end of the song) and playback speed for music;
  - a song's details: where it plays from, its key, where it's saved.

## Design
- **Model (Rust):**
  - `SavedTrack` gains `added_at` (unix ms; favorites and playlist items already store it), for Recently added and the Date added column.
  - `CollectionPlayRequest` gains `order: Option<Vec<String>>` (track keys in the order shown), so a sorted table plays as shown.
  - `Settings::track_tables` (`TrackTablePrefs`) holds, per table kind (`favorites`, `playlist`, `library`, `album`, `artist`, `history`, `search`):
    - `sort` (`TrackSortKey` custom/title/artist/album/added/duration, and descending);
    - `view` (list/compact);
    - `columns` (ordered `{id, visible, width}`: album, added, duration, plays, source).
  - `Settings::panels` (`PanelPrefs`): rail width (72 = covers only, up to 360), side panel width (280–560) and the full player's split (30–70 %).
  - Saving: `collections_saves(track)` gives `{favorite, playlistIds}`; `collections_set_saves(track, saves)` applies the difference in one transaction.
  - Queue: `PlaybackControl::Move { item_id, before_item_id }`, `PlayNext`/`AddToQueue` (enqueue after the current item, or at the end), `SetRate { rate }` (an engine command for all three engines) and `SleepTimer { minutes | end_of_track | off }` in the playback core.
  - Artist page: `music_artist(name)` gives popular tracks (the providers' search for the name, keeping the songs whose artists include it, deduplicated by key), library albums and library tracks.
- **UI:**
  - `music/TrackTable.tsx`:
    - its header has sortable columns, dnd-kit to reorder, borders to drag for width, and a ▾ Columns menu as a popover;
    - rows have artist links, the Plus, a hover play button, and a right-click menu; selection drives a bulk bar;
    - filtering is a search field in the table's toolbar.
  - `collections/SaveButton.tsx` and `collections/SaveSheet.tsx` (the popover). They replace `FavoriteButton` in the bar, panel, full player and rows.
  - `music/ArtistLinks.tsx` splits artist credits into links to `/artist?name=`; `/library/artist` redirects there.
  - `shell/ResizeHandle.tsx` (`role="separator"`, arrow keys): the rail, side panel and full player use it.
  - The rail shows Favorites and playlists under the tabs; covers only when narrow.
  - The video dock takes pointer events while it's showing.
  - Shortcuts live in `player/usePlaybackKeys.ts`, ignored while typing:
    - Space play/pause; Ctrl+←/→ previous/next; Shift+←/→ seek 5 s; Ctrl+↑/↓ volume;
    - M mute, Alt+Shift+S save, S shuffle, R repeat, Ctrl+L lyrics, Ctrl+Alt+Q queue.
  - `engine/useMediaSession.ts` sets the webview's Media Session (metadata, artwork, position, action handlers).
- **Order:** the core sorts in the UI (lists are already loaded whole); Rust receives the order only when playing.

## Decisions
- **Saving:**
  - The Plus saves to Favorites at once (Spotify's behaviour) and opens the popover. Done sends what should be true (`collections_set_saves`), and Rust applies the difference in favourites and playlists.
  - A check shows once the song is saved anywhere.
  - Title favourites (manga, videos) keep their heart: they're a library mark, not a playlist.
- **Song tables:**
  - Sorting and filtering happen in the UI over the loaded list. Playing sends the shown order (`CollectionPlayRequest::order`; `in_order` keeps duplicate songs as copies).
  - Each table kind keeps its look in `Settings::track_tables`; saved columns are merged with the defaults, so new columns appear.
  - Shift-click ranges read their anchor before React runs the update.
  - Narrow windows show title, artists and length; the columns appear from 768 px.
- **Artists:**
  - Credits split on `,`, `&`, feat./ft./featuring, `x` and `and`. The page also accepts the whole credit, so a duo like "Simon & Garfunkel" finds its songs.
  - Popular songs are the sources' search for the name, keeping songs they credit, agreed songs first (`search::artist_tracks`).
  - Names inside a row's button are link-role spans (a real link can't sit inside a button).
- **Right-click menu:**
  - One host replaces the webview's menu everywhere.
  - A component puts its items on the native event (`openContextMenu`); the host adds the spot's (fields, selected text, links, images) and the page's (Back, Forward, Reload, Full screen).
  - It is keyboard-driven, themed with the accent, and closes on scroll, resize or blur.
- **Playback:**
  - Speed is an engine command (`SetRate`) sent after each load. The audio element keeps it as `defaultPlaybackRate`, the YouTube page re-applies it when a video plays, and Media3 keeps it.
  - The sleep timer's minutes run as a task with a generation number, so a newer setting cancels it. "End of song" loads the next one paused.
  - YouTube songs show their artwork until the video is asked for, and the video can be clicked while it shows. It is clipped to the panel when the panel scrolls.
  - Shortcuts are off inside the readers.
  - Desktop has Media Session (Windows' media overlay and media keys).
- **Sources:**
  - Turning a source off goes through the host's `ContentFilter`: searches, music search and stream lookups skip it. Source lists still show it, dimmed, to turn back on.
  - Auto-loading waits at least the cooldown between pages and keeps a button when it's off.
- **The crash-free extension path:**
  - Nuclear conversions record the repository's version, and declare provider kinds found in the code.
  - The shim skips an undeclared provider instead of failing the plugin.
  - The packer keeps a published version's bytes and warns, rather than replacing them.
  - Install rows say Installed for a moment.
- **Windows:**
  - The shell's build script watches the icons: tauri-build didn't, so the exe kept its old icon.
  - The Windows file system ignores case, so `contextMenu.ts` and `ContextMenu.tsx` (and `trackTable.ts`) were renamed apart.
