# M46.16 — A player you can trust, and polish around it

## Goal
- **Pause always pauses; play always answers.**
  - The core holds a pause (`held`): the state says Paused at once.
  - A song still being found loads paused.
  - An engine that starts anyway is paused again, and buffering while paused doesn't look like playing.
  - Play shows Loading at once.
  - A load that hasn't started after 25 s counts as the stream failing, so the next stream or the next song plays: no stuck player.
  - Downloaded copies already play first (M46.10).
- **The playing mark:** Spotify's bouncing bars beside the playing song (still while paused) and its title in the accent colour, in song rows, the song table (in place of the number) and chart cards. Songs are matched by title and artist, since a song comes from many sources.
- **Reopening:**
  - The queue, its order, the song and the position are saved every 5 s, device-local.
  - On the next start, once the UI's engines listen, they come back paused there (Settings → Player → "Pick up where you left off", on by default).
  - Links through the old run's proxy go as lookups, and artwork goes as its source address.
  - Desktop website windows now store where they are on every finished load and when the window is left or closed, with the webview's own address, so single-page sites count too. Android's browser already did.
- **Text:** the app's text isn't selectable by mouse; fields, `.selectable` text and error details are.
  - Right click copies the words under the pointer ("Copy “Thriller”").
  - Songs add Copy title and Copy artist.
  - Lyrics have "Make lyrics selectable" / "Stop selecting lyrics" in their right-click menu.
- **The miniplayer window** opens in the app's background colour (a canvas turns the CSS colour into `#rrggbb` for `background_color`), and its title, artist and times grow with the window.
- **Other media:** a video (the player, either platform), a trailer, or read-aloud pauses this device's music, unless "Keep music playing with other media" is on (off by default).
- **Side panel:** About the artist and Credits cards under Now playing; the panel scrolls with no scrollbar.
- **Podcasts' right-click:**
  - Episodes: Play or Resume, Play next, Add to queue (`podcast_queue`), Mark played/unplayed, Download/Remove download, Show/Hide notes, Go to the show, Copy title or show name.
  - Shows (followed and found): Open, Check for new episodes, Follow/Unfollow, Copy name or feed address.
- **Discord presence (desktop):**
  - Off by default. It shows "Listening to <artist>", the song, its cover (the source's https address) and a progress bar, and nothing while paused.
  - Updates at most every 4 s, plus a seek. If Discord isn't running, it tries again every 30 s.
  - It needs the ID of a self-registered Discord application (Settings → Connections → Discord). Raygun has none of its own, and borrowing another app's would show that app's name.

## Decisions
- **`PlaybackCore::held`:**
  - Set by `pause` and by loads without autoplay; cleared by `play` and by loads with autoplay (skips, a tapped song).
  - `PlaybackService::on_resolved` loads with `autoplay && !held`.
  - The watchdog (`watch_start`) arms only once a load reaches an engine, so slow lookups aren't cut short; they have their own provider timeouts.
- **`PlaybackCore::restore`** keeps a saved shuffle order when it is a permutation of the items, else queue order. It runs only into an empty queue and only once per run (`playback_restore`, called by the shell 600 ms after mount, when the engines listen).
- **The text rule** is CSS on `body` (`user-select: none`) with `input, textarea, [contenteditable], .selectable` allowed. The context menu's copy item takes the closest text element's words, up to 300 characters.
- **New settings:**
  - `keep_music_playing` (false), `resume_on_start` (true);
  - `discord: DiscordPrefs { enabled, app_id }`;
  - `audio_quality: AudioQualityPrefs`, used by M46.18.
- **`other_media_started`** pauses with `control_here`, so music playing on another device (Connect) plays on.
- **Discord** uses `discord-rich-presence` 1.1 on its own thread, fed from `PlaybackService::send`, with `StatusDisplayType::State` ("Listening to <artist>"). Phones get a stub: they have no Discord IPC, and a user token mustn't be used.
- **Window zoom** already scales text (WebView zoom). The text that didn't scale was the miniplayer's, now sized in `vw` with `clamp`.

- A load without autoplay (reopening, the sleep timer's end of song) starts as **Paused**, not Loading. An engine that loads paused reports nothing, so a restored song otherwise showed "Finding a source…" with a pause button (found on Windows). Pressing play on a loading song arms the same 25 s watchdog.

## Verification
- **Tests:**
  - core: the pause holds through a late engine start and buffering, play answers at once, skipping clears the hold; a saved queue comes back paused with its order, and a broken order falls back;
  - `last_played`: files kept, proxy links as lookups, whole seconds;
  - `discord`: presence follows playback, is resent only on a new song or a seek, and is cleared when paused;
  - UI: the playing mark in rows, paused versus playing; `playbackRestore` called once; podcast episode menu (Resume, Play next, Add to queue, Mark as played).
  - Also lint, type-check, all app tests (409), clippy, `cargo test --workspace`.
- **Windows (release build):**
  - The Ghostbusters card in Popular now showed the bars beside its rank and its title in the accent.
  - Right click on "Popular now" offered Copy “Popular now”, and dragging over Recently played highlighted nothing.
  - The side panel scrolled to About the artist (Ray Parker Jr.'s photo, Detroit · 1954–) and Credits, with no scrollbar.
  - The miniplayer opened already dark.
  - The lyrics' right-click "Make lyrics selectable" let a drag select two lines, and "Stop selecting lyrics" turned it off.
  - After pausing Monster Mash at 0:04 and quitting, Raygun reopened with it paused at 0:04, Play shown, and the playing mark in Recently played. Play went on from 0:04.
- **Android (Pixel_4 emulator):**
  - The ListenBrainz chart's list showed the bars and the accent on "SWIM" while it played.
  - After pausing, force-stopping and relaunching, the bar showed "SWIM" paused with its progress kept, and Play went on.
- **Not checked live:**
  - Discord presence: Raygun has no Discord application ID, and Discord isn't signed in here.
  - A stuck engine's 25 s skip: covered by the code path only.
