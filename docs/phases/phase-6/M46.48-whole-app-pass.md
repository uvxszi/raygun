# M46.48: whole-app pass

A pass over every area of the app: bugs, missing basics, error wording, performance with many sources, and new pieces (incognito, Miru, lyrics translation, permissions, download notifications).

## Goal and design, by area

- **Search and Browse**:
  - **Search pills:** they come from one list (`search/searchTypes.ts` `searchLink`). Audio gains an Audiobooks pill (`search/AudiobookSearch.tsx`), and `/audiobooks` redirects there.
  - **Relevant:** a mix of rows that load independently: songs, artists, albums, playlists (featured first), podcasts, audiobooks.
  - **Music search:** streams each source's answer as it arrives (`SearchProgressEvent`, `search/searchStream.ts`).
  - **Recent searches:** `recent_searches.rs` (`PrivacyPrefs::search_history`).
  - **Extensions pills:** All · Installed · Not installed · Updates.
  - **Repositories:** list what they hold as chips (`extensions/RepoContents.tsx`, `extension_repo_preview`). The "What Raygun can run" page is removed.
  - **Text fields:** have a clear button (`shell/TextField.tsx`).
- **Errors and many sources**:
  - **Error mapping:** `raygun-net::issue` turns an error into one sentence naming the source, followed by the raw detail. `ext-host/failure.rs` reads a script's error together with what its requests met (browser checks, rate limits, site changes). The UI reads these with `lib/issue.ts` in `shell/SourceIssue`.
  - **Extension lanes:** extensions run on 2–4 lanes, so one busy extension no longer blocks the others.
  - **Request limits:** requests run on a network runtime with limits of 6 per site and 32 in total, and calls nobody waits for are aborted.
  - **Fan-out:** `ext-host/fanout.rs` asks sources in health order. Failing sources rest for 1–15 minutes, and identical asks share one call.
- **Stremio**:
  - **Protocol:** `stremio/protocol.rs` follows stremio-core: ids encoded as `encodeURIComponent` (including `:`), `/manifest.json` replaced while keeping queries, extras with skip first, and the legacy `/stremio/v1` transport.
  - **Stream ids:** `Meta::stream_ids_for_video` is the one place stream and subtitle addons get an id they know.
  - **Details and health:** details come from the first addon in order, with a grace period. Addon health is kept, and addons can be turned off and reordered (migration 0034).
  - **Addons page and Discover:** the addons page shows chips of what each addon provides. Discover is at `/watch/catalog`.
  - **Torrent-only streams:** folded under a count, with a copy action.
- **Shosetsu**:
  - **Repositories:** they are named from their address, and a repository page leads to its index. Missing libraries come from other added repositories.
  - **Requests:** Shosetsu's User-Agent, 300 ms spacing per host, and `Retry-After`.
  - **Scripts:** many Jsoup, OkHttp and Lua compatibility gaps closed. Script filters and settings are mapped, and failures are written in plain language (`shosetsu/errors.rs`).
  - **Loading:** scripts load in parallel and are released when idle.
- **Miru**: `raygun-ext-host::miru` runs Miru's JS extensions:
  - **Index and packages:** `index` and `package` read and install them.
  - **Runtime:** `runtime` + `prelude.js` provide Miru's API.
  - **HTML queries:** `dom` and `xpath` run them on the Jsoup engine.
  - **Types:** bangumi, manga and fikushon map to video, manga and novel sources. Torrent videos are listed but never played.
  - **Network safety:** extensions allowed any public host only resolve to public addresses.
- **Repositories**:
  - **Suggested:** Paraline (`uvxszi/paraline-raygun` root `index.json`: Raygun's packages by absolute URL, community sources, fixed Shosetsu scripts), Shosetsu's and Nuclear's.
  - **Old URLs:** retired URLs fold into Paraline on start (`raygun_db::extensions::replace_repos`).
  - **Index format:** a Raygun index may mix formats.
  - **Packing:** `pnpm repo:publish` packs every extension (`raygun-ext pack-all`).
- **Comics sources**:
  - **New built-ins:** WEBTOON, Tapas, MANGA Plus Creators, Naver Webtoon, Internet Archive comics (public domain), Pepper&Carrot and xkcd.
  - **MangaDex 1.3.0:** language select, latest by language, original-language filter.
- **Song identity and imports**:
  - **One identity:** `collections::identity` (mirrored in `music/trackKey.ts`) is used everywhere.
  - **Duplicates:** `playlist_add` skips songs already in a playlist unless asked. Migration 0031 plus `collections::repair` merge old duplicates.
  - **Picked sources:** they play at once (`track_use_source`). `resolver::to_try` gives cooled-down matches one try per load, and `player/SkipNotice.tsx` says why a song was skipped. Picking a source can also adopt its details.
- **Song downloads**:
  - **One list:** `music/songDownloadCache.ts` is patched from `SongDownloadProgressEvent`, sent at most 4 times a second.
  - **Queue:** runs by `queued_at` (migration 0033), with reorder, pause and retrying all failed downloads.
  - **Errors:** `error_detail` holds the raw detail.
  - **Source picker:** lists each source's answers (`music/sourceAnswers.ts`).
- **Player**:
  - **Watchdog:** `PlaybackCore::watchdog` nudges an engine that says it plays but doesn't advance, or never starts. Native Android has its own watchdog in the plugin.
  - **Fewer redraws:** position-only states are sent at most once a second, and views select the parts they need (`usePlayerSelect`, `useLiveValue`).
  - **Sizes:** taken from the room available, with `coverOffset`/`widthOffset`/`lyricsOffset`.
  - **Queue editing:** `player/QueueList.tsx` and `PlaybackControl::ClearUpcoming`.
  - **Artists:** artist names in the bars are plain text, and the handle closes the phone player.
- **Android system**:
  - **Background playback:** `background.rs` mirrors playback into the plugin's `MirrorPlayer` while the webview plays. `WebAwake` keeps the webview running off screen, and system controls go to Rust.
  - **Settings → Permissions:** `permissions.rs`; each permission is asked once per reason.
  - **Download notifications:** `notifications/` and `DownloadNotices.kt`.
- **Video player**:
  - **Track languages:** audio follows the device's languages (`VideoPlayerPrefs::audio_language`, `original`), and `SubtitleMode` decides when subtitles show. Languages are matched by `PlayerLanguages.kt` and `video/trackPicking.ts`.
  - **Loading:** `PlayerLoading.kt` is a custom ring with buffering and source notes.
  - **Rotation:** holding the rotate button locks the exact orientation.
  - **Errors:** `PlayerProblem` puts failures in plain words.
- **Video seasons**: `raygun-resolver::video_match` (TS mirror `watch/videoMatch.ts`) reads seasons from titles, file names and stream names, Roman numerals included. It is used for:
  - watching order and specials;
  - the stream check;
  - AniSkip;
  - Local Files grouping;
  - duplicates;
  - Find in other sources.
- **Continue and refreshes**:
  - **Continue rule:** `catalog::continue_index` is the one rule: the episode touched last, or the next one once it's finished, with extras skipped. Continue watching keeps one entry per title (`video::latest_per_title`).
  - **Refreshes:** `shell/useLiveInvalidation.ts` maps Rust change events to cache refreshes.
- **Podcasts and audiobooks**:
  - **Titles:** tidied when shown (`podcasts/titles.rs`).
  - **Episode order:** sort and filters per show or book, with its own speed (`podcasts/order.rs`, migration 0032).
  - **Book search:** the audiobook directory asks every source, ranks and dedupes (`podcasts/directory.rs`, `AudiobooksFoundEvent`).
  - **Lit2Go:** new, with provider-listed chapters.
  - **Home:** Continue listening.
- **Lyrics translation**:
  - **Translations from sources:** providers return translations and romanizations (KuGou 1.1.0 reads KRC). They are kept and matched by line (`lyrics_translation/`, migration 0036).
  - **On-device:** OPUS-MT on candle (`raygun_ai::marian`).
  - **AI translators:** AI providers translate in one request.
- **Comics and books readers**:
  - **Webtoon view:** keeps only nearby pages in memory, tracks the current page correctly, and pinch-zooms.
  - **Tap zones:** Mihon's layouts.
  - **Reading mode:** a default reading mode (migration 0035).
  - **Chapters:** chapter selection and a download menu.
  - **Books:** included in library updates.
  - **Novel reader:** themes and justified text.
  - **Imports:** EPUB and CBZ are streamed.
- **Local files, websites, Downloads, Updates**:
  - **Scans:** report progress; missing folders keep their items; Android access is asked for.
  - **Website blocker:** per-site ad blocking fixed.
  - **Downloads and Updates:** retry and cancel everywhere, and Updates is one timeline by day.
- **Home and menus**:
  - **Hide and not interested:** "Don't show this artist" and "Not interested" (`Settings::hidden`, `hidden.rs`), and hiding Home rows.
  - **Menus:** one order for every card and row (`music/mediaMenu.ts`).
  - **Home rows:** new rows (top artists, new episodes, followed releases, popular podcasts).
  - **Artists in rows:** plain text on touch screens.
- **Settings**:
  - **Navigation:** sections with search and Reset to defaults.
  - **Saving:** optimistic, serialized saves with visible failures.
  - **Settings from elsewhere:** settings arriving by sync or restore apply at once.
  - **Sizes:** follow the screen (`settings/useApplySize`), and every size setting is a `PrefSize` offset row.
- **Incognito**: `privacy.rs`. While it is on, no history is written: plays, recents, progress, searches and stamps. It ends with the app unless `PrivacyPrefs::keep_incognito` is on.
- **Docs**: specs, roadmap and README are written neutrally, and release notes list what a release contains.

## Verification

- Rust: `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings` and `cargo test --workspace` pass on the merged tree, bindings check included.
- UI: eslint, `tsc -b` and the full vitest suite pass.
- Live checks run by area:
  - `stremio_live_survey` (33 of 63 usable addons fully work, and no failure left is Raygun's);
  - `shosetsu_survey` (reference scripts 23 → 25 of 60; every remaining failure is the site's);
  - `miru_survey` (145 install, 144 load);
  - `comics_live`, `librivox_live`, `loyalbooks_live`, `lit2go_live`, `book_titles_live`, `kugou_live`, `marian_live`, `aniskip_live`, `paraline_live`.
- Not verified on a device in this milestone: Android background playback, permission prompts, download notifications, the video player's Kotlin changes (compiled only), and reader gestures.
- Known gaps:
  - Swiping the app from Recents still ends playback, because Tauri exits with its last window.
  - Torrent streams are listed but never played.
  - MANGA Plus (API refuses non-browser clients) and bot-checked comics sites are not included.
