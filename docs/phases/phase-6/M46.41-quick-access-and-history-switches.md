# M46.41 — Quick access fills itself; history can be off

## Goal
- **Quick access** is the sidebar's pins (M46.29) and the phone's Your library row.
  - Anything added to the library is pinned too: comics, books, videos, playlists (made or saved), followed podcasts, audiobooks, followed artists, saved albums.
  - Taking it out of the library unpins it, unless it was pinned by hand.
  - `Settings::rail.auto_add` (default on) and `Settings::rail.limit` (default 20, 1–1000). Past the limit the oldest automatic pins go; pins made by hand are never dropped automatically.
  - Settings → Library → Quick access: the switch and the limit.
- **History switches** (Settings → Data → Privacy):
  - **Keep listening history** off: plays aren't recorded (no history, no play counts, no Made for You taste from new plays). What's there stays until cleared.
  - **Keep recently played** off: Home's Recently played and Your music's "played from" aren't recorded or shown; video Continue watching isn't affected.
  - `Settings::privacy` (`PrivacyPrefs { history, recently_played }`, both on by default), applied in Rust (`CollectionsService::record_play`, `add_recent_context`).

## Verification
- Windows: a playlist made by Merge was pinned in the sidebar by itself. This found a race (M46.45): the sidebar dropped a new playlist's pin when the pin arrived before the refreshed playlist list; it now only drops playlists it saw before, and the UI no longer pins new playlists itself (Rust does, with the switch and the limit).
- Tests: the Rust settings tests for `RailPrefs` defaults and limits, Rail and Layout UI tests.
- Not checked by hand: the history switches on a device (Settings → Data → Privacy; covered by the Home tests).
