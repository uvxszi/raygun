## Goal
- **Song menus:**
  - A downloaded song says **Remove from downloads** (the menu knows by the song's key, `music/trackKey.ts`, the resolver's `track_key` in TypeScript).
  - A song with an album has **Go to album** before Go to artist: a file on this device opens its library album; otherwise `music_find_album` looks for the album among the sources (same core title, the song's artist), else Search → Albums.
  - On phones the ⋮ sheet carries the rest of the long-press menu (play next, queue, download, cover, source, edit, go to, copy) under its own actions.
  - **No doubled copy items:** the words under the pointer aren't offered when the menu already copies (Copy title, Copy artist), and a long press on a touch screen shows only the item's own options (no Back, Forward, Reload, Full screen).
- **Explicit music:**
  - Settings → Browse → Content has **Show explicit music** next to the 18+ switch (`Settings::explicit_music`, on by default).
  - Sources mark songs (`OnlineTrack::explicit`): Deezer's `explicit_lyrics`, YouTube Music's E badge. Search rows show an **E**.
  - With it off, the host drops explicit songs from every list a source gives (search, albums, charts, genre rows, recommendations and so mixes), and 18+-rated music sources are hidden.
  - The 18+ switch no longer touches `music.*` providers (`ContentFilter::allows`).
- **Artists' discography:** artist pages get **Discography**, with chips All · Albums · Singles and EPs · Compilations · Live · Other.
  - Releases come from `music.metadata` providers' new `artistReleases` (Deezer: the artist's albums by `record_type`; MusicBrainz: release groups by primary and secondary type), merged in `ExtensionService::artist_releases`.
  - Only releases credited to the artist are kept, editions folded into the plain title with the first year, newest first. `MusicAlbum::kind` (`ReleaseKind`) carries the kind.
- **Discord:** the presence (M46.16) is removed: setting (old rows are ignored, `settings::RETIRED`), service, dependency and Settings card.
- **Podcasts card:** in Search's All and Relevant it's a grid of covers with titles (`PodcastResultList grid`).
- **Lyrics and captions with trims:**
  - Synced lines the trims cut off are left out (`sound::within_trims`, the line already showing at the start kept), so lyrics show only what plays. Captions do the same.
  - **The start trim moves the lyrics:** changing it shifts the lyrics offset by the same amount, so a video's cut intro takes its seconds off studio-timed lyrics. "Move the lyrics with the start" turns that off (on by default, per device).
- **Lyrics sources:** Fix the lyrics → **Lyrics from your sources** lists every lyrics provider's version (`lyrics_options`, synced first, with the first line); the pick becomes the song's own lyrics.
- **Hear the words with AI:** the same editor transcribes the song with the speech setup from M46.32 (`lyrics_transcribe` → `CaptionService::transcribe_song`): its downloaded copy, else a stream a source has (YouTube's player gives no sound to hear). The lines become its synced lyrics.
- **Captions source:** the captions card switches between the feed's transcript and AI ("Transcribe with AI instead", "Use the feed's transcript", `captions_use_feed`; `Captions::has_feed`).
- **Covers from other sources:** Edit details → **Find covers in your sources** (`detail_cover_options`): covers the music sources have for the song, its album or the artist, picked as the edit's cover.

## Decisions
- **No Discord token sign-in:**
  - Discord's only presence route without a registered application is to act as the user's account (a self-bot), which its terms forbid.
  - The alternative is one Raygun application registered by the project owner, its id built in, so no user ever enters one. Desktop only (local RPC). It's left for the owner to decide.
- **Explicit is per song, not per source,** so a clean source isn't hidden for one track, and the 18+ filter stays about images and writing.
- **Saved songs don't keep the explicit mark:** favorites and history store no such field; the filter applies to what sources give.
- **A lyrics pick is an edit:** choosing another source's lyrics saves them as the song's own, so they stay the same offline and can be fixed; "Use the source's lyrics" goes back.
