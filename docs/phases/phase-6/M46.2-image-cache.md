# M46.2 — Image cache and cache settings

**Asked by the user (2026-11-01):** "for extension icons and other shows and thumbnails, the important ones cache them so users dont have to keep loading them, and update when needed … and have cache settings and such".

**Relevant docs:**
- the M46.2 survey (a subagent over this repo and `reference/`), summed up under Decisions;
- M27 (the page cache), M24 (the image proxy), M41 (Stremio), M13/M32 (repositories, Shosetsu);
- `docs/research/mihon.md` (cover cache, chapter cache, storage settings).

## Goal
- **Covers kept on disk:** covers, posters, thumbnails and icons that people see again and again open from disk, even offline.
- **Library covers never removed:** the library's covers stay cached whatever the size limit.
- **Covers that change update:** copies are checked again in the background after a set age.
- **Extension icons:** shown where repositories list them.
- **Settings → Data → Storage:** each cache's size against its limit, Clear, the limits, the refresh age, and emptying the page cache on start.

## Design

### The cover cache (`raygun-net::cover_cache`, served at `/cover/<token>`)
- **Kept apart from reader pages** (`/image/`, the page cache), so reading a long chapter never pushes covers out.
- **Files** are named by the image's token: SHA-256 of its address and headers, the same in every version. (It replaces `DefaultHasher`, which Rust doesn't promise to keep stable.) Check records live in `meta/`: when the copy was last checked, its ETag and its Last-Modified.
- **Serving:** a copy on disk is served at once.
  - Past the refresh age, it's checked in the background with `If-None-Match` / `If-Modified-Since`: a 304 updates the record, a 200 replaces the copy.
  - Offline or on errors, the copy stays.
  - One image is checked once at a time.
- **Kept covers** live in `keep/` and are never evicted. The rest are trimmed least recently used first, to 90% of what the limit leaves after the kept ones.
- **What's kept:**
  - library titles (manga, novels, videos, saved Stremio titles) on library, update and title pages;
  - followed podcasts;
  - installed extensions' icons;
  - installed addons' logos.
- **The sweep:** 30 s after start, kept covers of titles no longer in the library move back among the others.
- **A manual title refresh** drops the title's cover copy, so it's fetched again (Mihon's `manualFetch`).

### What goes through it
- **Covers in every listing:** manga, novel and video-source covers (listings, title pages, library, updates).
- **Podcasts:** art for subscriptions, show pages, episodes and search.
- **Video:** download posters.
- **Stremio:** posters, backgrounds, logos, episode thumbnails, catalogue rows and addon logos (they were loaded straight from the web before).
- **Continue watching and history posters.**
- **Stored addresses:** where a poster is stored (watching progress, download rows), proxy links are mapped back to their source with `ImageLinks::original`, since a proxy link doesn't outlive the app.

### Extension icons
- **Repository entries** gain `icon` (relative to the index or absolute; http(s) only). Shosetsu's `imageURL` maps to it.
- **Browse → Extensions** shows icons through the cover cache.
- **At install** the icon's address is remembered (`local_value` `extension.icon.<id>`). Installed extensions show it as a kept cover, and their sources in Browse → Sources show their extension's icon.
- **An icon that fails to load** falls back to the letter tile.

### Settings (`Settings::cache`, `CachePrefs`, one `cache` row)
- **Covers and icons cache:** 128 MB – 4 GB, default 512 MB.
- **Reader page cache:** 128 MB – 4 GB, default 512 MB (the old fixed size).
- **Check covers again after:** never, 1, 3, 7 (default) or 30 days.
- **Clear the page cache on start:** off by default (Mihon's option).
- **Changes apply at once:** limits and the refresh age change without a restart.

### Settings → Data → Storage (`storage_usage`, `storage_clear`)
- **Covers and icons, reader pages:** used of the limit, with a bar and Clear.
- **Shared files:** size and Clear.
- **Music artwork:** size only; it comes back only with a rescan, so it isn't cleared.
- **Downloads:** size only; they're removed in Downloads.

## Out of scope
- **Online music artwork** (search results, saved tracks, the player): their addresses are saved with tracks and playlists, so routing them needs the music milestone's stream cache (M52).
- **The webview's own HTTP cache and site data:** M46.3 (Websites) adds clearing web data.
- **Custom covers:** M47.

## Decisions
- **Survey (2026-11-01):**
  - Raygun cached covers and reader pages together, in one 512 MB pool keyed by `DefaultHasher`, with no revalidation.
  - Stremio posters, history posters and addon logos were loaded straight from the web.
  - No extension icons were shown at all.
  - Nothing could be cleared or limited.
  - Mihon keeps library covers in a lasting cover cache and refreshes a cover on a manual refresh or a URL change, with "Clear chapter cache" and its size in Data settings. CloudStream sizes a Coil disk cache. RiPlay lets users pick the cache size and clear it.
- **Two caches, not one:** covers are seen again and again and are small; pages are read once and are large. Separate limits keep a long chapter from pushing the library's covers out.
- **Kept covers live in their own folder,** not in a table, so the eviction pass (shared with the page cache) never sees them. The library changes rarely; a sweep 30 s after start sorts covers in and out, and the library and title pages mark covers as kept when they show them.
- **Revalidation runs in the background:** a cover opens at once from disk and the next view gets a changed image (stale-while-revalidate). This is cheaper than checking on every view, and works offline.
- **Stable names:** cache files are SHA-256 of address and headers (`proxy_image_token`), so a new Rust release never orphans the cache.
- **Stored posters stay source addresses:** `VideoEntry.poster` and download rows used to store whatever the request carried (now often a proxy link). `ImageLinks::original` maps a proxy link back before storing; history and continue watching link them through the cache when shown.
- **Extension icons:**
  - in the repository index (`icon`, or Shosetsu's `imageURL`) rather than inside packages, so Browse can show them before installing;
  - the address of an installed one is kept in `local_value` (no migration needed for this one value per extension);
  - built-in extensions show their letter until the repository's index lists icons.
- **Music artwork isn't clearable,** since it comes back only with a rescan, and online music artwork stays as it is (out of scope).
- **The proxy's port changes per launch,** so the webview's own cache never hit across restarts. The disk cover cache is what makes covers instant after a restart.

## Verification
- **Checks:**
  - `pnpm lint`, `pnpm type-check`, `pnpm test` (296 UI tests, among them `settings/Storage.test.tsx`);
  - `cargo test --workspace` (the cover cache with a local server: disk copies, 304, a changed image picked up in the background, kept and released covers, `sync_kept`, clear, unsafe tokens; check records; Shosetsu icons and icon address resolution);
  - `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`;
  - bindings current.
- 📱 **On the Pixel_4 emulator:**
  - **Browsing MangaDex** filled `cache/covers` with 24 covers and their check records.
  - **Kept covers:** with Chainsaw Man and Frieren in the library and NovelFull installed, the sweep after the next start logged "library covers sorted … kept_in=3", and `keep/` held those three.
  - **Offline:** with Wi-Fi and data off and the app restarted (a new proxy port, so nothing from the webview's own cache), Library → Comics showed both covers.
  - **Settings → Data → Storage:** "Covers and icons 4.6 MB of 512 MB" with a bar; pages, shared files, music artwork and downloads with their sizes. Clear on Covers emptied the folder ("0 B of 512 MB"). The limits, "Check covers again after 7 days" and "Clear the page cache on start" showed under Caches.
  - **Extension icons:** with the Shosetsu repository added, Browse → Extensions showed NovelFull's, Syosetsu's and Rulate's icons. After installing NovelFull, its icon showed in the installed list and on its source in Browse → Sources. Built-in extensions showed their letters.
  - **Stremio:** Cinemeta's catalogue posters loaded through the cache (58 files after one screen).
  - **Clean-up:** app data cleared, and the network restored.
- **Bugs found on the device and fixed:**
  - library covers were kept only once a library page linked them again (the sweep now moves them in too);
  - the sweep would have released installed extensions' icons and addons' logos (they now count as kept).
- **Not seen on the device:**
  - a cover changing on its server (covered by the test with a local server);
  - eviction at the limit;
  - a manual refresh fetching a cover again;
  - clear-on-start.
