# M46.15 — Connect: play on another device

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):** "on mobile it shows you devices like this, you can just click the one that isnt this device and at the bottom of the screen it will say playing on (device name) and you can essentially remote control is also from the device you are on" (with Spotify's Connect sheet).

**Relevant docs:** M20 (the LAN remote: server, pairing, the API), M21 (device sync's mDNS discovery), M41.1 (Android 17 local network access), M46.13 (the Connect button).

## Goal
- **Devices:**
  - The Connect sheet lists other Raygun devices on the network, found by mDNS (`_raygun-connect._tcp`). A device can also be added by its address (`192.168.1.20:8820`), which works where multicast doesn't (the emulator, guest Wi-Fi).
  - A device can be played on when its "Let other devices play here" is on. That switch starts the LAN remote's server, which serves Connect too.
- **Asking first:**
  - The first time, the device being played on shows "Pixel 4 wants to play on this device", with Allow and Don't allow.
  - Allowing pairs the two: the asking device keeps a token, as a paired remote browser does, and later connections need no question.
  - The paired device appears in Settings → Remote's device list and can be forgotten there.
- **Playing there:** tapping the device moves playback to it:
  - the queue (local files go as lookups, with their title and artist), the song, the position, and shuffle; it plays if it was playing;
  - this device then pauses.
- **Remote control:**
  - While connected, the bar shows "Playing on <device>". The bar, the full player and the queue show that device's song, position and queue.
  - Every control and every new song (play, pause, skip, seek, volume, queue edits, playing a song or an album) goes to that device. Rust's `PlaybackService` hands them to Connect instead of its own core.
- **Coming back:** "Play here" on this device takes the queue and position back and pauses the other device; "Disconnect" leaves it playing there.
- **Losing the device:** when its events stop for good, the bar says so and this device is in charge again.

## Design
- **Target side (`raygun-remote`):** new routes beside the remote page's:
  - `GET /api/device`: the name, id and kind, without a token;
  - `POST /api/connect/hello`: asks this device's user. It waits up to 60 s for an answer and returns a token when allowed. Only one question is open at a time.
  - `GET /api/connect/events?token=`: SSE with `state` (PlaybackState) and `queue` (QueueSnapshot) events, each sent when it changes;
  - `POST /api/connect/command`: a `ConnectCommand` (replace, enqueue, enqueue next, control).
  - It is advertised by mDNS while the server runs.
- **Controller side (`raygun-app/src/connect.rs`, `ConnectService`):**
  - devices: found, added and paired, kept device-local;
  - the session: the target, its last state and queue, and an SSE task that emits `ConnectEvent`;
  - the transfer; "Play here".
  - Queue items cross devices with portable sources and artwork. Artwork goes as the source address and comes back through each side's own cover proxy.
- **UI:**
  - `connect/useConnect.ts`: the status, devices and actions;
  - `useNowPlaying`, `usePlaybackState` and `useQueue` show the session's state while connected;
  - the reworked Connect sheet, the "Playing on" strip, and `connect/ConnectPrompt.tsx` (the question on the other device).

## Decisions
- **One server for both:** Connect uses the LAN remote's server (M20).
  - "Let other devices play here" is the remote switch, shown in the Connect sheet with this device's address.
  - An allowed device is paired the way a browser is, so it appears in Settings → Remote and can be forgotten there.
  - Asking needs no QR code. `POST /api/connect/hello` waits up to 60 s for the question on this device (`ConnectRequestEvent`; the desktop window comes forward). Only one question is open at a time (429 otherwise).
- **The transfer** is one `ConnectCommand::Replace`:
  - it carries the queue in item order, the current item, the position (a playing song's position plus the time since the last report), shuffle and whether it played; repeat follows as a control;
  - local files and links only this device reaches go as lookups by title and artist;
  - artwork goes as its source address (`ImageLinks::original`) and comes back through the receiver's own cover cache.
- **Redirect, not mirroring:** while connected, `PlaybackService`'s public queue and control calls (`enqueue`, `replace`, `replace_from`, `enqueue_next`, `control`) go to a channel that posts them one by one to the other device.
  - So every way of playing something (search, albums, playlists, the remote, keys, Media Session) plays there with no UI changes.
  - The device's own commands use `apply`/`control_here`, which aren't redirected.
- **Following:** one SSE stream (`/api/connect/events`, checked every 300 ms) sends `queue` only when the queue changes and `state` when the state does. The UI's `usePlaybackState`/`useQueue` take the session's state and queue while connected, so the bar, the full player, the side panel and the cards show the other device.
  - After the stream drops, it's retried three times (2, 4, 8 s), then the session ends: "Lost <device>", and this device is in charge again.
  - A 401 means the other device forgot this one: its token is dropped, and the next connection asks again.
- **"Play here"** takes the other device's queue and position back (paused if it was paused) and pauses it there. "Disconnect" leaves it playing on its own.
- **Finding devices:** mDNS `_raygun-connect._tcp` with `id`, `name`, `kind` in TXT, on one shared daemon.
  - Android holds a `WifiManager.MulticastLock` while browsing (`CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE`).
  - Typing an address (`/api/device`, default port 8820) covers networks where multicast doesn't reach, and the emulator.
- **Local network access:** reaching a device asks Android 17's permission first through the same gate as addons, with its own reason (`connect`).

## Verification
- Tests:
  - `raygun-remote`: hello allowed and refused, command auth, events sending queue then state, then only what changed;
  - `raygun-app::connect`: addresses, SSE parsing, the transfer with portable sources and the position;
  - `connect/Connect.test.tsx`: devices, playing there, the strip, Play here, adding by address, the question.
  - Also lint, type-check, all app tests (406), clippy, `cargo test --workspace`, and the Kotlin plugin compiled.
- **Phone → PC** (Windows release build and the Pixel_4 emulator):
  - On the PC, the Connect sheet's "Let other devices play here" showed `192.168.0.19:8820`.
  - On the phone, "Add a device by address" with `10.0.2.2:8820` showed the local network explanation and Android's dialog; after Allow, "Windows PC — Asks on that device the first time" was listed.
  - Tapping it showed "Android phone wants to play on this device" on the PC; after Allow, the PC played "Housewife" from the phone's position with the phone's queue next.
  - The phone's bar showed "Playing on Windows PC" and the laptop icon. Pause on the phone paused the PC, and swiping the bar moved the PC to "Choosin' Texas".
  - "Play here" (the phone's row) paused the PC and loaded the queue on the phone. Its only stream source had nothing for "Choosin' Texas", so it went on to the next song, as it does when nothing is found.
  - Afterwards both pairings were forgotten and the PC's switch was turned off.
