# M46.26 — Song lengths and cover images

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):**
- "for some reason lots of tracks just dont show their duration, fix that"
- "clicking a song image or album image you can show a popup and download as image types into wherever you want, when downloading a song also include the image for offline, if from an album then only once so you dont have duplicate files"

## Why lengths are missing
Several sources don't send a length: Apple's charts, ListenBrainz, some tag-song lists and Nuclear plugins. Lists built from them (charts, genre pages, mixes, and songs saved or played from them) keep the gap, because a song's length was only ever what its first source said.

## Goal
- **Raygun learns lengths and remembers them** in a device-local table `local_track_duration` (track key → ms, with where it came from). It learns from:
  - playback: the engine's duration once a song has played a few seconds (a stream's length is the truest, but only within 15 s of what a source said, so a music video's intro doesn't replace the song);
  - every song list a source returns that has lengths (search, charts, albums, genre and mood rows);
  - lookups: `track_durations(tracks)` answers what it knows and queues the rest for a background lookup. That searches the `music.metadata` sources one song at a time (title and artist must match) and emits `TrackDurationsEvent` as lengths arrive.
- **Song tables fill gaps:** `TrackTable` (every saved list, charts, albums, mixes) asks for the lengths it lacks and shows them as they come. The playing song's length is stored once it has played a few seconds.
- **Saved songs get their lengths too:** favorites, playlists and history missing a length have it filled in place. This is device-local: other devices learn lengths their own way.
- **Cover images:**
  - Clicking the artwork opens a sheet with the image large, its size, and "Save image". This works in the full player, the side panel and the album page; song menus get "Save cover image".
  - Save offers PNG, JPEG or WebP, and where: Pictures/Raygun or a folder you pick (the same folder picker as saving songs, which doesn't change where downloads go).
  - Raygun asks the source for its largest version (Apple's `…/600x600bb` → `3000x3000bb`, Deezer's 1000 px, YouTube's `maxresdefault`, Last.fm's original), falling back to the shown one.
- **Downloaded songs keep their cover for offline:**
  - When a song finishes downloading, its cover goes into the image cache's kept part (`keep/`, never evicted), and the downloaded copy shows that kept cover, in lists and while playing.
  - Songs of one album share one cover address, so one file. Covers are keyed by the source address, and Deezer and Apple album art is per album.
  - Songs written out as files carry the cover in their tags (as before).

## Decisions
- **Lookups are polite:** one at a time, 2 per second at most, and at most 200 queued. Each song is asked once per run, and failures are remembered for a day.
- **Which length wins:** a stored length from a source is kept; the table fills only gaps. Playback can correct a stored length only when they're within 15 s.
- **Image formats:** the `image` crate encodes PNG and JPEG (quality 92). WebP is lossless (the crate's encoder). An image already in the asked-for format is saved as it came, unchanged.

## Verification
- **Tests:**
  - lengths fill gaps, playback corrects them, and a miss never hides a known length (`raygun-db`);
  - covers: hosts' largest versions (Apple, Deezer, YouTube) and PNG/JPEG/WebP conversion (an image already in the format is kept as it is);
  - UI: a table row without a length shows one once the lookup's event arrives;
  - UI: the side panel's artwork opens the cover sheet, and saving as PNG to Pictures/Raygun calls `cover_save`.
- **Windows (release build):**
  - Clicking the side panel's artwork opened the cover sheet: the image large, 300 × 300 px, JPEG/PNG/WebP and Pictures/Raygun or a folder.
  - Save as PNG wrote `Pictures/Raygun/Jane Austen - Pride and Prejudice.png`, a real 300 × 300 PNG. The test file was deleted afterwards.
  - The song from Local Files showed its length (0:20) in the table.
