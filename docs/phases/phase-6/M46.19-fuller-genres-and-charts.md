# M46.19 — Fuller genres and more kinds of charts

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):**
- "also add more sources for seeing like more types of trending and stuff, and the genres Browse all menu has genres but lots of them are empty and just doesnt have much data at all".

## Why the genre pages were empty
- A genre's page showed only charts whose `tag` matched it, and only Deezer tagged its charts (27 genres).
- So every other genre and every mood had nothing from the sources, only the user's own tagged songs.

## Goal
- **Songs for any genre or mood.**
  - `music.charts` providers may answer `tagSongs({tag, aliases, kind, region, limit})` with songs and a name for the row. The host asks every provider that has it at once (12 s each, as interactive calls).
  - The genre's page shows a row per source that answered, above the user's own songs.
  - A failing source gets a calm issue card, not a red error.
  - Sources:
    - **Deezer:** the genre's chart (28 genres), else a Deezer radio (moods: Happy, Sad, Chill, Focus, Party, Workout, Romantic, Sleep, Energetic; genres such as House, Techno, Indie, Disco, Bossa Nova), else Deezer's own playlist named after it, else the fullest listener playlist with the name in its title.
    - **Apple Music:** the iTunes Store's top songs for 27 genres in the user's country.
    - **ListenBrainz:** tag radio: recordings listeners tagged with the name or its other spellings, the better known ones first.
    - **Audius:** the genre's trending songs this month.
- **More kinds of charts**, grouped:
  - Charts carry a `group`: `top`, `trending`, `new`, `genre`, `mood`, `decade`, `underground` or `viral`.
  - Browse → Music lists them under headings in that order, and Browse all's Charts tiles show the main kinds (not genres).
  - New charts:
    - Apple's genre charts (8 shown as tiles, all 27 on genre pages).
    - Deezer's decades ('60s–2000s radios).
    - ListenBrainz's Fresh singles (the last two weeks' singles people play most).
    - Audius's Trending, Underground trending, All-time and 12 genre charts.
- **Audius** (new first-party source): the open platform where artists upload their own music. No key, only an app name.
  - Charts as above; search (songs play in full); `music.stream` candidates at 320 kbps.
  - Gated songs (supporters or buyers only) are left out.

## Decisions
- `tagSongs` sits on `music.charts`, not a new kind: every source with charts knows its genres, and the host already gathers charts by provider.
- The host passes the vocabulary's aliases (`genres::aliases`) so sources match their own spelling. Apple and Audius map names to their ids; ListenBrainz sends up to five spellings as one OR query.
- The app caches each tag's rows for 30 minutes per region, like chart songs.
- Groups are optional for third-party sources: a chart without one counts as `genre` when it has a tag, else `top`.
