# M46.39 — Playlist search, featured playlists, more lyrics sources

**Asked by the user (2026-11-02):**
- "also add in playlists search and featured playlists, or make featured as a tag pill"
- "kreate and rimusic have these, also their lyrics sources and other sources, find them and add them in"

## What Kreate and RiMusic use (read from their source, `reference/Kreate`, `reference/RiMusic`)
- **Search and catalogue:** YouTube Music's web API (innertube): songs, videos, albums, artists, **community playlists** and **featured playlists** (search filters `EgeKAQQoAEABagoQAxAEEAoQCRAF` and `EgeKAQQoADgBagwQDhAKEAMQBRAJEAQ%3D`), playlists by `browse` with `VL<id>`.
- **Lyrics:** LRCLIB (Raygun has it), **KuGou** (`mobileservice.kugou.com` song search, `krcs.kugou.com` lyric search and download, LRC in base64), and **YouTube Music's own lyrics** (`next` → the Lyrics tab's `browseId` → `browse` → `musicDescriptionShelfRenderer`).
- **Streams:** Piped and Invidious instances, which hand out YouTube's stream files directly.
- **Discord:** Kizzy, signing in as the user's account.

## Goal
- **Playlist search:**
  - Search → Music gets a **Playlists** pill with a **Featured** chip: every `music.metadata` provider that answers `searchPlaylists({query, limit, featured})`.
  - Each playlist opens `/music/playlist` with its songs: Play, Shuffle and **Save to library** (a Raygun playlist with the same name and songs).
  - YouTube answers with YouTube Music's community and featured playlists; Deezer with its public playlists (`/search/playlist`, `/playlist/{id}`; featured = Deezer's own editorial ones).
- **Lyrics sources:** a new first-party extension **KuGou** (`lyrics`, in the official repository, one tap to install), and YouTube gets a `lyrics` provider (YouTube Music's lyrics, plain). They show up in "Lyrics from your sources" (M46.36) and fill in when LRCLIB has nothing; synced lyrics win.

## Decisions
- **No Piped or Invidious streams:** they fetch YouTube's stream files outside its player, which YouTube's terms forbid. Raygun keeps playing YouTube through its embed (M08) and records it when asked (M46.10).
- **No Discord sign-in:** same reason as M46.36 (a self-bot).
