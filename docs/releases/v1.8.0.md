Raygun 1.8.0 reads PDFs and maths, looks up words as you read, streams torrents, fixes the video player's crashes and captions, adds site scripts for websites, skips sponsored parts of YouTube videos, and brings many new open sources to Paraline.

## What's in this release

**Video player fixes**
- Opening Sources no longer crashes the player.
- White captions are white, even over subtitle files with their own colours, and the custom colour preview follows the colour picked.
- AI subtitles show one line at a time instead of two on top of each other.
- Captions glide above the controls instead of shrinking. Second subtitles have their own tab, long lines wrap within a width you can set, and caption backgrounds have rounded corners.

**Reading**
- PDF books: pages drawn sharply at your screen's size, chapters from the book's outline, a text view, search and reading aloud. PDFs open from Local Files, Add files and Open with.
- Maths in books shows as formulas (MathML and TeX), at the reader's text size.
- Long-press a word to look it up: pronunciation, meanings with examples, synonyms, origin and translations, from Wiktionary and the Free Dictionary API. Words looked up before open offline.
- Search inside a book.
- Generated book covers show the author.
- On desktop, the reader's bars stay within the reader instead of covering the side panels.

**Video**
- Torrents: sources that answer with torrents or magnet links play in the video player, with seeking. Off until you turn them on in Settings → Video player → Torrents, Wi-Fi only by default, and nothing is shared after watching unless you allow it.
- SponsorBlock skips sponsored and non-music parts of YouTube videos and songs, with Undo. Each category can be set to Skip, Show or Off.
- YouTube plays the original audio track over dubbed ones, and H.264 can be preferred where other codecs struggle.

**Websites**
- Site scripts: extensions can add scripts and styles to websites opened in Raygun's browser, and offer the page to Raygun (play, queue, download). Each script can be turned off per site.
- Websites keep playing in the background, with notification and lock-screen controls.
- YouTube enhancements, adapted from NouTube, is available as a site-scripts extension to add by its address.

**Sources**
- Sources that need a key or a sign-in are listed together in one line, and aren't asked anything until they're set up.
- New in Paraline: ListenBrainz radios and recommendations; OpenStax, Wikibooks, BCcampus, Saylor, Green Tea Press and GNU manuals; the Library of Congress; ESA's Hubble and Webb videos; MIT OpenCourseWare lectures; and, with your own key, Last.fm, Europeana, the US National Archives and the National Park Service.
- More Mihon extensions convert and run, including ones that draw PDF pages.
