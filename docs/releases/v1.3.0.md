Raygun 1.3.0 keeps the music going in the background and after Raygun is closed, gathers every source in one place, and adds backups by category, Open with and Share, a folder browser for Local Files, AI subtitles for videos, AI layers in the comic and book readers, and text size and font settings.

## What's in this release

**Playback**
- Music keeps playing when Raygun is swiped away from the recent apps or left with Back: the next song loads, the lock screen's controls work, and opening Raygun again shows the player as it is. A song in YouTube's player moves to another source that has it, or pauses with a notification saying why.
- YouTube songs keep playing in the background and with the screen off.
- A song whose stream stops working looks for another source before it's skipped, and the player says so. A skipped song offers Find a source.
- Songs only YouTube's player plays can be saved by recording them as they play, in real time, after Android asks.
- Downloaded songs show the length of their file.

**Sources**
- Raygun starts with no sources and suggests Paraline. Sources say which repository they came from. Devices that used an earlier version keep their sources: they install from Paraline on the first start.
- Sources holds repositories, Stremio addons, servers and extensions on one page, with pills by kind and by media, Audio included.
- One Add works out what an address is: a Stremio addon, a repository (Raygun, Shosetsu, Nuclear, Miru, LNReader, Kodi or Mihon), one extension or a server. GitHub project pages work too, and a file or folder can be chosen instead.
- Mihon repositories can be added: Raygun converts each comic extension when it's installed, about three in four of them, including sources that unscramble or decrypt their pages. The ones it can't convert say why.
- A source whose search finds nothing is matched against its own listings, marked "From its listings". Settings → Browse sets how far it looks.
- Solving browser checks by hand (experimental) opens the request that met the check and takes the pass back to the source.
- Repositories can list Stremio addons, which install as addons. Paraline lists Stremio's OpenSubtitles v3, Cinemeta and WatchHub.
- A Data pill gathers sources of details, tags, charts, lyrics, subtitles and ratings. ListenBrainz charts, in Paraline, gives the songs most listened to this week, month, year and of all time.

**Data and backups**
- Backups by category, to Download/Raygun or a folder you choose, found again wherever they are, showing what each holds before restoring.
- Settings → Data lists every category with counts and sizes, and clears each on its own.
- Erase everything (type ERASE) starts Raygun again as new; backups stay.

**Open with and Share**
- Videos, songs and playlists other apps open with Raygun play in its players. Songs opened from a web address are named by their tags.
- Shared links and words go to Search, where a YouTube link shows its card; a Spotify link opens Import songs.

**Local Files**
- Raygun's own folder browser reaches the top of the storage and Download, which Android's picker refuses.
- Add files imports comics and books, and copies songs and videos into Raygun.
- Access to all files is optional, for EPUB and comic archives in any folder.

**Video**
- The subtitles panel adds Transcribe with AI and Translate subtitles, each shown as its own track while the video plays.
- The player's top bar moves the window on desktop, as the app bar does.
- Episode and source lists open at the one playing, not at the top.

**Comics and books**
- Both readers have a chapter list, sorted and filtered as on the title's page.
- The webtoon page counter shows the page being read, and swipes turn pages in the paged reader on phones.
- Book paragraphs indent by an amount, and reading aloud moves to the chapter opened.
- An AI button (bottom right, above the chapter buttons) opens AI layers: Upscale and Restore lines for comic pages, with Anime4K models, and Translate for both, on the device. Layers run from the bottom up, each with its own model and switch, so one translation can feed another.
- Reading aloud's controls fit phones and sit above the chapter bar.

**Library and lists**
- Quick access lists the whole audio library by itself, by recently played, recently added or name, with pins on top. Settings → Quick access sets what it shows.
- Song tables pick rows by their covers. Items have a ⋮ menu with their own actions, copy included, instead of a right-click or long-press menu; quick access keeps its own.
- Downloads lists every comic, book and video with downloads, with their actions and export.
- Rows a mouse drags sideways glide on.

**Appearance**
- Text size, interface size and the device's fonts, for the whole app and the book reader.

**Websites**
- The Android browser has tabs; popups never open one.
- Downloads show a bar with progress and Open, and a finished file's notification opens it.

**Notifications and devices**
- Release notifications open the album or single they announce.
- Connect's sheets have larger buttons and rows.
- The player's side panel fits its contents to its own width, and credits lose stray quotes.
- On phones, the player bar swiped down slides under the tabs.
