Raygun 1.6.0 adds podcast directories and new open sources, shows edited and found covers everywhere at once, makes translation and transcription start where you are, quiets notifications to the ones that matter, and tidies 18+ filtering.

## What's in this release

**Sources (in Paraline)**
- Podcast search works again: fyyd (no account needed) and Podcast Index (with your own free key, entered in the source's settings).
- Incompetech: Kevin MacLeod's music under CC BY, to search, stream and browse.
- Live Music Archive: concerts by artists who allow sharing them, as albums.
- Gutenberg audiobooks: thousands of public-domain books read aloud.
- More Mihon extensions convert and run.
- Subsonic, Jellyfin, Emby, Audiobookshelf and OPDS servers can be added from Sources → Add, and sources that need a server say so.

**Covers**
- A cover changed in Edit details shows at once in the player bar, the full player and the notification, even while the song plays.
- Playlist covers load in the order you see them, and a cover host that doesn't answer no longer holds others back.
- Songs whose cover shows in a list show it in the player too.

**Lyrics and AI**
- Translation starts at the line playing and shows lines as each batch is done; transcription starts where the song is and shows lines as they're heard.
- New online providers: MyMemory (free, no account), LibreTranslate (your server's address) and a Whisper server for transcription.
- Delete saved lyrics removes transcribed lyrics too.

**Search and the library**
- The "No 18+" pill shows only while 18+ content is on in Settings, starts switched on, and hides 18+ sources' results everywhere, Relevant included.
- Explicit songs follow the setting alone; the "No explicit" pill is gone.
- While 18+ content is off, the library says how many entries it hides and why.

**Notifications**
- Only news makes a sound: followed artists' releases, new chapters and episodes of what's in your library, and new Raygun versions. Each opens what it announces, and Settings → Notifications has a switch for each kind.
- Downloads, installs, exports and playback notify silently.

**On phones**
- Swiping the player bar throws the song off the screen and brings the next or previous one in from the other side.
- Check downloads has Keep as it is, and its explanations sit above the buttons.

**Also**
- Crossfade is 2 seconds by default on new installs.
- MusicBrainz shows an active artist's first year without a dash.
