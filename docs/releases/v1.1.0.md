Raygun 1.1.0 is a pass over the whole app: fixes across every area, many sources handled calmly and quickly, and new features.

## What's in this release

**Playback**
- Music keeps playing in the background on Android, including songs that play through YouTube.
- A song that shows as playing but stays silent is detected and restarted.
- The player redraws far less often: smoother on phones, sized from the screen, with a landscape layout.
- Artist names in the player bars are plain text. A tap on the handle closes the phone player.
- Queue editing: drag, swipe to remove, clear upcoming, save as a playlist.

**Library and songs**
- One identity for every song, so imported songs stay in their playlists. Adding a song that's already there asks first; old duplicates are merged.
- A source picked for an unavailable song plays at once and can bring its title, artist, album and cover. A skipped song says why.
- Select several songs to favorite them all. Songs only drag in edit mode.
- Screenshot imports join song titles and artists that OCR split into separate rows.
- Song downloads: one queue with reorder, pause and retry, accurate progress, and notifications.

**Search and sources**
- Search answers from each source as it arrives; failing sources rest and go last.
- Error messages name the source and say what happened.
- Audiobooks pill, and a fuller Relevant view with playlists, podcasts and audiobooks.
- Clear buttons on text fields, and recent searches (off while incognito).
- Stremio addons follow Stremio's protocol, so far more addons work, TMDB included. The addons page lists what each provides.
- Shosetsu compatibility improved, Miru extensions supported, and one Paraline repository.
- New comics sources: WEBTOON, Tapas, MANGA Plus Creators, Naver Webtoon, Internet Archive comics, Pepper&Carrot and xkcd.
- Optional, off by default: open a site to pass its browser check yourself (experimental).

**Video**
- Audio in the device's language, subtitles when the audio isn't.
- Seasons recognised in every form ("II", "2nd Season", "第2期"), in the right order. Next episode skips specials.
- Continue watching resumes the episode watched last.
- A new loading indicator; hold the rotate button to lock the exact orientation.

**Podcasts, audiobooks, lyrics**
- Clean audiobook and chapter titles, sorting and filters per show, speed per show, Continue listening.
- Lit2Go audiobooks; LibriVox search through its own catalogue.
- Lyrics translations from sources, on the device, or with an AI provider.

**Comics and books**
- A lighter webtoon reader with pinch zoom and Mihon's tap zones; a default reading mode; chapter selection.
- Books get library updates; novel reader themes.

**Everything else**
- Incognito mode.
- Settings → Permissions, and Settings search with reset per section.
- Sizes follow the screen, with sliders and number fields.
- "Don't show this artist", "Not interested", and the same menus on every card.
- Local files: scan progress, and missing folders keep their items.
