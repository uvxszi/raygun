Raygun 1.9.5 comes to Linux, macOS and more Windows devices, runs in a web browser, and adds sound from other sources and live picture effects to the video player.

## What's in this release

**More platforms**
- Linux: AppImage, deb and rpm for x64 and ARM64, deb and rpm for RISC-V, Flatpak, and Arch packages (CachyOS included).
- macOS for Apple silicon and Intel, and Windows for 32-bit PCs and ARM64.
- Updates find the right file for each system, and AppImages update themselves.

**Raygun on the web**
- Raygun runs in a browser tab at uvxszi.github.io/raygun. It keeps your library, settings and sign-ins in the browser and syncs with your other devices once linked.

**Video**
- Audio from other sources: an episode's other streams offer their sound tracks, so you can watch one source with another's audio. An audio delay field lines it up, like the captions delay.
- Picture effects while watching: brightness, contrast, saturation, filters, deflicker and denoise. Smart audio evens out loud and quiet parts and makes speech clearer.
- The quality and source playing are highlighted in their menus.
- Movie and series pages have Find in other sources, Stremio addons included.
- Series show their seasons again; long-running ones pick a season from a list.
- Video cards are wide (16:9), with posters and a compact list as view options. Cards can play a muted trailer when you point at them, or as they pass the middle of a phone's screen.
- Back from a title returns to the list at the same place, with everything you'd loaded still there. Playing straight from a card opens the title's page under the player, so Back goes there first.
- Show only playable asks the sources again when turned on.
- The player follows the device's rotation by default.
- A caption choice carries to the next video, other titles included, unless that title has its own.
- On Android the status and navigation bars show while the player's controls are up.
- Sources that list each episode as a quality or a server now show proper episodes, with the qualities inside each one.
- Saving a video downloads from one source, the one you played last unless you pick another.
- Deleting a downloaded video also deletes its folder, and folders left over from earlier deletions are cleaned up.

**Library and sources**
- Library → All: pick several items at once and act on them together. Hold an item on a phone, or right-click it on a computer, for its menu.
- Sources reopens on the tab you left, scrolled where you were.
- The extension catalogue can reload all repositories, one repository, or check one extension for an update.

**Reading aloud**
- "St. Paul" is read as "Saint Paul", and other common abbreviations are said in full.
- A new option splits long sentences at commas (off by default).
- The speed button still steps on a tap; hold it or right-click it for speed choices and a slider.
