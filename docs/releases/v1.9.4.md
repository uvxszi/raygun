Raygun 1.9.4 runs Kodi add-ons, adds Nuvio providers, and widens Stremio addon support.

## What's in this release

**Kodi add-ons**
- Kodi video and music add-ons install from a Kodi repository and run inside Raygun: their folders open as pages, and their videos and streams play in Raygun's players.
- Each add-on is listed once, in the version made for this device. Add-ons that do nothing in Raygun (skins, services, TV-screen clients) say so instead of installing, and the libraries an add-on needs come with it.
- Add-ons run in a sandbox with no files or network of their own; everything goes through Raygun, so Offline Mode and the network rules apply.

**Nuvio providers**
- Nuvio provider repositories can be added in Sources → Add. Each provider installs as a source, and the streams it finds for a film or an episode join the other sources in Choose a source and the player's Sources sheet.

**Stremio addons**
- Addons that list other addons open as a list of addons to add.
- Genres, cast and directors from addons' links, release years, trailers given as streams, and TV channels with a guide play with what's on now.
- A stream's country limits show beside it.

**For extension authors**
- A new `video.streams` kind gives streams for a film or an episode by its TMDB or IMDb id, with a guide, a template and validation.
