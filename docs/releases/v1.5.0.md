Raygun 1.5.0 plays the next song at once, resumes downloaded songs instantly, keeps covers as JPEG XL, converts nearly every Mihon extension, runs every Nuclear plugin without running out of memory, and makes downloads and recordings more dependable.

## What's in this release

**Playback**
- The next and the previous songs' sources are found while a song plays, so Next, Previous and a song's end play at once.
- Sources that played a song are remembered and asked first next time; songs matched when a playlist was imported keep their match.
- Play and Pause flip the moment they're pressed. A downloaded song or a file on the device resumes at once, with no loading ring; streams show it only while they really wait.
- No more distorted moment as a song starts or after a seek, and a downloaded song next in the queue no longer pauses at the change.
- HLS streams play and download as songs (SoundCloud's, for one).

**Downloads and recordings**
- A song downloads from the source it's playing from, or the one it played from, before any search.
- Recording is the last resort: every way to a file is tried first, then the download is tried again later, and only then is a song recorded. Recordings show their progress in a notification with Stop.
- Several downloads: the notification has Pause, Skip and Cancel all, and Downloads → Music can remove songs from the list.
- "Needs attention" no longer comes back on songs that were downloaded again.
- AI models, FFmpeg, yt-dlp and the other tools Raygun fetches can be cancelled while they download, from the app or the notification.

**Sources**
- Mihon extensions: about 95 in 100 of a store's extensions now convert, whole themes included, and many more list, open and read.
- Nuclear plugins no longer run out of memory on large pages, every plugin in Nuclear's registry loads, and slow ones keep working in the background instead of failing.
- Search's Custom sources is a grid of switches that stays quick with hundreds of sources.
- Long lists (the extension catalogue, sources, song tables) open and filter quickly with thousands of entries.

**Covers and storage**
- Covers are stored as JPEG XL, converted once (most without any loss), and take about a fifth less room.
- Playlist covers made of four always show all four.
- Songs without a cover get one from your sources in the background, while Raygun is idle.
- Cached data is kept in tiers: pictures go first, small records much later.

**On phones**
- The recording bar sits above the player bar and minimizes to a round button showing how many songs are left.
- Closing the full player by swiping down is smooth while a song plays.
- Quick access shows bigger covers; the equalizer no longer stops a scroll that passes over it.
- The song title in the full player has the whole width above its buttons.

**Also**
- Continue watching offers Download, or Delete download once it's downloaded.
- Settings → AI's on-device models can be filtered by use and sorted.
- A new scrollbar style shows bars while scrolling and fades them out after.
- Artists still active show their first year without a dash after it.
