Raygun 1.2.0 ships only public-domain and openly licensed sources. It adds a guide and tools for writing extensions, connections to your own media servers, more plugin formats, Play on TV, and many fixes to menus, downloads and selection.

## What's in this release

**Sources and content**
- Built-in sources are public-domain or openly licensed only: Internet Archive (public-domain films, openly licensed music, public-domain comics), Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Wikisource, LibriVox, Loyal Books, Wikimedia Commons, NASA, Blender's open movies, Pepper&Carrot and xkcd.
- Internet Archive results are checked per item: a film is listed only when it is old enough to be public domain or is marked public domain, and music only from netlabels and Musopen under a Creative Commons or public-domain licence.
- MusicBrainz gives song, album and artist details, genres, discographies and cover art.
- Paraline is the only suggested repository, and it lists only public-domain and openly licensed sources. Repositories and addons already added stay.
- New installs come with OpenSubtitles as the only Stremio addon.
- Screens that need a kind of source you don't have (charts, lyrics, podcast search) say so and link to Extensions.

**Your own servers**
- Connect Jellyfin, Emby, Subsonic or Navidrome, Audiobookshelf, and OPDS catalogues (Komga, Kavita, Calibre) under Browse → Sources → Servers.
- Their music, videos, audiobooks, comics and books open in Raygun's own players and readers. Sign-ins are kept encrypted on the device.

**Extensions and other formats**
- A guide to writing Raygun extensions, with a page for each kind of source and examples built on public-domain sites.
- `raygun-ext init --kind` starts an extension from a template, `raygun-ext validate` checks it before publishing, and `raygun-ext test` runs one call in Raygun's own extension host.
- LNReader novel plugins install from their repositories.
- Kodi repositories can be browsed; their add-ons are listed but can't run yet.
- Nuclear plugins, Shosetsu scripts, Miru extensions and Stremio addons still work when added by address.
- Every extension, source and addon has an on/off switch, and Select picks several to turn on or off at once, with Select all.
- Sources, extensions, repositories and addons can be marked 18+ or not by hand.

**Video**
- Play on TV: Google Cast and DLNA receivers on the network, or a TV added by address.
- Video cards play from their play button and open the title elsewhere; holding one shows its options.
- Download a range of episodes, several titles at once, or download and then export. Pick subtitle languages when downloading; your system's language is picked first.
- Subtitle delay and style come first in the subtitles panel.
- Video sites' pills sit in the Videos row of Search.

**Comics and books**
- Find in other sources shows each match's chapter or episode count, sorts matches, and can migrate a title to another source.
- Covers and backdrops open close up, with larger versions, zoom, share and copy.
- Sources whose search finds little are also matched against their own listings.

**Library and shell**
- Hold to select results in Search and Sources; add them to groups, folders and favorites, or download them together.
- Back returns to where you were scrolled.
- Playlists, charts, favorites, podcasts and audiobooks show their total length.
- Favorites, Downloaded and Local files have menus, and can be pinned again after unpinning.
- Choose the tab Browse opens on.
- Music and video player settings have their own sections, and each player opens its own settings.
- Website downloads stay in Raygun, in the Downloads folder or one you choose.
- Download notifications end when their downloads do, and carry Raygun's mark.
- A Smart Chips button beside the mascot's, coming soon.
