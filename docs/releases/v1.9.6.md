Raygun 1.9.6 lets Raygun on the web use your phone or computer's Raygun as its back end, and brings sources to the web version.

## What's in this release

**Raygun on the web**
- Link the web version with a Raygun app on the same network, and the browser uses that app's sources, searches and catalogues, in every format the app supports. Songs and videos the app finds play in the browser, with covers and subtitles coming through the link. Your library and settings stay in the browser and sync as before.
- Turn it on or off in Devices → Sources from your apps; Browse shows which device answers.
- On its own, the web version now installs the sources that work in a browser, and lists the rest as needing the app.
- Books and videos from those sources work in the browser: browsing, search, title pages, the book reader, the video player, the library and Continue watching. Home shows charts from music sources.
- Buttons for things a browser can't do are hidden, or point to linking an app.
