Raygun 2.0.0 brings Smart Chips, songs from any number of screenshots, video sources picked by more than quality, sound controls for everything you play, and files shared between your devices.

## What's in this release

**Smart Chips**
- Smart Chips are features you add when you want them: the Smart Chips button lists them with what they do and their size, downloads them, and turns them on or off.
- Smart Shuffle: the shuffle button gets a third state that mixes recommended songs into the list, about one for every three of its own, chosen from what the list sounds like. Suggested songs carry a sparkle, with Add to this playlist and Not for me.
- Smart Subtitles: compares the subtitle tracks of a language from different sources and puts the ones that agree first, marked Matching.
- Smooth Motion is now a Smart Chip. It makes in-between frames for smoother movement, and says when a video already has a frame for every screen refresh.

**Importing songs**
- No more 100-song limit: pasted lists, CSV files and screenshots import every song. Spotify playlists over 100 songs import in full with your own Spotify API keys in Settings → Import.
- Reading screenshots shows its progress, and the review stays open until you close it.
- Tap a cover or screenshot to look at it closely, crop it, and read it again.
- New text readers: PaddleOCR on your device (much more accurate, including Japanese), your AI providers' vision models, and Windows OCR. Try another reader from the review, downloading it in place. A newly downloaded reader works without restarting.

**Video**
- Sources are judged by length as well as quality: trailers and short "not available" clips are passed over, a source that plays well stays, and a broken one moves on to the next instead of going back. A clearly better source arriving early takes over where you are.
- Sources has Leave out trailers and Leave out short videos, and a button on each source to turn it off or open its settings.
- Subtitles from other sources show, including files numbered in ways the player used to reject. The subtitle delay changes at once, also while paused, before the start, past the end and at any speed.
- The Effects button is now "Fx". Effects add tempo, pitch and the video's own equalizer.
- Remove a watermark: draw a box round it, or let Raygun find one that stays through scene changes and confirm it.
- Messages say when a video's server is busy instead of calling the link expired.

**Music and sound**
- Pitch, tempo and the equalizer work for everything you play: songs, podcasts, audiobooks and videos.
- Songs in Downloads → Music play on a tap, with Play, Play next and Add to queue in their menu.
- The phone's full player has a Devices button beside Lyrics.

**Devices**
- Linked devices can send each other downloaded songs, chapters, episodes and picked covers, and keep song edits and lyrics in sync. Choose what goes to each device in Devices.
- Scan a barcode from Search: a CD's finds its album, a book's ISBN finds the book.

**Everywhere**
- Icons keep their size and sharpness at every interface and text size, and the video player follows the app's sizes.
- Background lookups are gentler on sources: they wait while you use the app and never call one source over and over.
- Raygun on the web can add sources by address.
