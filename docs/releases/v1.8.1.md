Raygun 1.8.1 fixes the crashes that could make 1.7.0 and 1.8.0 close at every start, adds crash logs and a safe start, fixes video subtitles, and lets read-aloud pause and use offline voices.

## What's in this release

**Crashes**
- Raygun no longer closes when the page that draws its screens fails, which could happen at every start on devices with many sources. It shows a clear screen instead, with Try again, See logs, Start in safe mode and Clear caches.
- Saved pages restored at start are kept to a sensible size.
- A source that misbehaves (endless loops, huge answers, broken pictures or PDFs) fails on its own and can't take the app down.
- After a crash, the next start is a safe start: it skips what it restores at start, lets queues wait, and holds back a source that keeps failing during start.
- About → Crash logs lists what went wrong, to open, share or save. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Uninstalling on Android can keep the app's data, and the desktop uninstaller asks before deleting it.

**Video**
- Subtitles that come with a torrent are listed and play.
- Picking a subtitle while subtitles are off works on the first tap.
- Quality lists what sources actually offer, shown with their real sizes, and picks the nearest when an exact one isn't there.

**Reading aloud**
- The reader's button pauses and resumes from the same sentence; Stop is separate. The notification has the same controls and keeps reading with the screen off.
- Offline voices: download Piper or Kokoro voices in Settings → AI → Reading aloud, pick a voice per language, and preview it.
