Raygun 2.0.3 makes the built-in browser's downloads dependable, moves them to a Downloads button, adds a menu for holding links and pictures, and makes album barcodes find their album.

## What's in this release

**Browser downloads**
- Downloads no longer sit on "Waiting": they reach Raygun even when Android has paused or closed it while the browser was open, and finish once it's back. A download still waiting for Raygun says so and offers Open Raygun.
- Files made by the page itself (`blob:` and `data:` links) download too, under the name the site gives them.
- Downloads moved from the bar at the bottom to a Downloads button in the toolbar, with a ring showing progress. Its panel lists each download with Cancel, Retry, Open, Show in folder and Remove, and Clear finished.

**Browser menus**
- Hold a link or a picture for its options, as in other browsers: open it in a new or background tab, download it, share it, or copy its address.

**Barcodes**
- Scanning a CD's or record's barcode finds its album instead of ending in an error when several codes were looked up at once.
