Raygun 1.0.0 is the first release: music, podcasts, audiobooks, films and series, comics and books in one app, from local files and the sources added to it. It is built for Windows and Android.

## What's in this release

**Music**
- Search across every source, with songs, artists, albums and playlists; a queue with crossfade, a 10-band equalizer, loudness normalization and per-song trims.
- A library of favorites, sortable playlists with drag-to-reorder, followed artists and local folders, with imports from Spotify links, CSV files or screenshots.
- Downloads with retries and fallback to other sources; on Windows, yt-dlp for songs that only YouTube has.
- Charts, genres, moods, decades and Made for You mixes on Home and in Browse.
- Lyrics from several sources, kept offline, editable, or transcribed on the device.

**Podcasts and audiobooks**
- Podcast subscriptions, episode downloads, and captions from feed transcripts or speech-to-text.
- Audiobooks from LibriVox and Loyal Books, played chapter by chapter.

**Video**
- Stremio addons, video-source extensions, YouTube and PeerTube, with downloads, resume, and trailers on title pages.
- A player with subtitles found in addons or made with speech-to-text, a typed subtitle delay, volume up to 200 % and a choice of external player.
- YouTube addresses typed in Search play directly; on Windows, yt-dlp downloads the video or its audio.

**Comics and books**
- Sources and local CBZ and EPUB files, a library with categories, chapter downloads, exports (CBZ, PDF, EPUB) and read-aloud.

**Devices**
- Device linking: linked devices keep the library, playlists, history and settings in sync, and either one can play on the other. A web remote works from any browser on the local network.

**Across the app**
- Extensions from Raygun's repository and others: Shosetsu novel sources, Nuclear plugins and Paraline Raygun.
- An MCP server for AI assistants, off by default.
- Websites in a built-in browser with an ad, tracker, popup and redirect blocker.
- A customizable layout, themes, accent colours and interface translations.
- Cached starts: lists and searches show at once and refresh in the background.
- In-app updates for the Windows installers and Android; the portable build reports when a new release is out.
