The first release of Raygun: music, podcasts, audiobooks, films, series, comics and books in one app, from your own files and the sources you add. For Windows and Android.

## Download

| You have | Get |
| --- | --- |
| Windows, installed (recommended) | `Raygun_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` — updates itself from inside the app |
| Windows, installed with an MSI (managed PCs) | `Raygun_1.0.0_x64_en-US.msi` — also updates itself |
| Windows, portable (USB stick, no install) | `Raygun_1.0.0_x64_portable.exe` — keeps its data in a `raygun-data` folder beside it |
| Android (any phone) | `Raygun_1.0.0_android_universal.apk` |
| Android, smaller download | `…_android_arm64.apk` (phones), `…_android_x86_64.apk` (emulators, Chromebooks) |

Windows needs Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 10 and 11 already have. On Android, allow your browser or file manager to install apps the first time; later updates install from Raygun itself (About → Updates).

## What's in it

**Music**
- Search every source at once, with songs, artists, albums and playlists; play anything, queue anything, crossfade, a 10-band equalizer, loudness levelling and per-song trims.
- Your library: favorites, playlists you can sort and drag, downloads (with fallbacks to other sources, and yt-dlp for songs only YouTube has on Windows), local folders, imports from Spotify links, CSV files or screenshots.
- Discovery: charts, genres, moods, decades and Made for You mixes, on Home and in Browse.
- Lyrics from several sources, kept offline, or transcribed on the device.

**Podcasts and audiobooks**
- Follow podcasts, download episodes, captions from feeds or transcription.
- Audiobooks from LibriVox and Loyal Books, played chapter by chapter.

**Watch**
- Stremio addons, video sources, YouTube and PeerTube; downloads, subtitles (found or made with AI), resume, trailers on title pages.
- Paste a YouTube address in Search to play it, or (on Windows) download the video or its sound with yt-dlp.

**Read**
- Comics and books from sources and your own CBZ/EPUB files: a library with categories, downloads, exports (CBZ, PDF, EPUB), and read-aloud.

**Your devices**
- Link your phone and computer once: they keep your library, playlists, history and settings in sync on their own, and either one can play on the other, Spotify-style. A web remote works from any browser on the network.

**Everything else**
- Extensions from Raygun's repository and others (Shosetsu novel sources, Nuclear plugins, Paraline Raygun), an MCP server for AI assistants, websites in their own ad-blocking browser, a customizable layout, themes and translations.
- Opens on what you saw last time: lists and searches show at once and refresh in the background.

## Good to know

- Android APKs are signed with Raygun's release key, so every later release installs as an update. A debug build installed earlier has to be uninstalled once first.
- The portable exe never updates itself: About → Updates opens this page when a new release is out.
