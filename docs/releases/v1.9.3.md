Raygun 1.9.3 fixes zooming in the comic reader, adds caption fonts, merges video search results, and keeps plugins that do nothing in Raygun from installing.

## What's in this release

**Comics and books**
- Double-tap zoom in the comic reader zooms at your finger and stays on a black screen, with the end of the chapter outside the zoom. A second double-tap fits the page again.
- Short strips, like Sandra and Woo's, start in the middle of the screen.
- Reading modes list Paged left to right above right to left; the extra reading mode button at the bottom on desktop is gone.
- PDF books have one Contents button in both page and text view, listing bookmarks and chapters.

**Video**
- Captions can use another font, for subtitles and second subtitles alike. The Style button now looks like one, with a sample.
- Tracks in the same language are told apart: "English (Forced)", "English 2", with where each comes from underneath. Audio tracks too.
- Search → Videos → Relevant shows one section per kind, with every source's titles together; a title several sources have shows once with how many have it. By source is one switch away.

**Sources**
- Plugins and addons that would do nothing in Raygun (mini players, scrobblers, themes, skins, services) show "Not for Raygun" with the reason and aren't installed. Ones already installed are marked, with Remove.
- Stremio addons with settings can be set up in Raygun; fields marked optional can stay empty.
