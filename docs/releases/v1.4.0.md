Raygun 1.4.0 converts video exports properly, runs Nuclear plugins that rely on yt-dlp on Android too, and adds selection across the library, a fuller Continue watching, smoother lyrics, a start screen and many fixes.

## What's in this release

**Exports and downloads**
- Videos export as MP4, MKV, M4A or MP3. A video whose picture or sound doesn't fit the format chosen is re-encoded, so every export plays in other apps; the export sheet says when a format re-encodes.
- Exports of comics, books and series can be limited to some chapters or episodes: all, unread, a range, or ticked ones.
- Stopping a download asks first, everywhere a download shows its progress.
- Downloaded videos have their full menu under Downloads: play, go to the title, mark watched, save as a file, share, AI subtitles and delete.
- The desktop bar's Downloads list scrolls, gives each item its own menu and shows the latest 20 (Settings → Downloads sets how many).

**Sources**
- Nuclear plugins that rely on yt-dlp now work, on Android as well as desktop: their search, streams and playlists are answered by Raygun.
- A playlist address typed in Search opens as songs when an installed source reads it.
- Sources has a tab with the extension catalogue of every repository.
- Search's Custom sources pill unfolds a switch for each source.

**Library and selection**
- Comics, books and videos in the library can be selected (hold, the circle on the cover's top left, or Ctrl/Shift-click) and acted on together, and their ⋮ menus work.
- Podcasts, audiobooks, episodes and chapters can be selected too: play next, add to queue, mark played, download, delete downloads, unfollow and pin.
- Continue watching can be selected and cleared in one go. Its ⋮ menu adds Play from the start, Next episode, Open page, Choose a source, Mark as watched, Download and Pin; removing asks first, and the X on each card is gone.
- Home's Quick access row shows quick access in its own order.
- Series still running show their first year alone, without a dash after it.

**Music**
- Lyrics glide from line to line, in the full view and in the phone's lyrics card.
- On phones, tapping the cover shows the lyrics, the lyrics button sits beside the download button, and the song's title has the whole width above its buttons.
- Covers open their save options on a hold (or a right-click), not a tap.
- Song menus: hold a title or name to copy it, one Go to artists item with their pictures, and taller rows. "Don't show" an artist is left out for songs that are saved, favorited or by a followed artist.
- Playlists in edit mode: holding the up or down arrow moves a song to the top or bottom. The selection bar stays in view while the list scrolls.
- Song lengths are kept for playlists, the lyrics timing can be typed, cover search shows the names it searches by, and a source found automatically is kept and its details offered.
- The equalizer's sliders can't be moved while it's off.

**Recordings**
- Songs waiting to be recorded share one queue, recording no longer needs screen capture, and songs can be played while one records. Recordings with gaps fill them in later; a copy with too much missing isn't played.

**Settings and start**
- Settings → AI is grouped by use: translation, transcription, text reading, online providers and on-device models.
- Raygun starts on a dark screen with its icon; What's new no longer flashes when it was already read.
- Cached data takes less room.

**Fixes**
- Notifications always have a title and text.
- Menus and sheets no longer open invisible after the page reloads.
- Desktop starts again (1.3.1 could close at start).
