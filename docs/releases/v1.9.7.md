Raygun 1.9.7 starts videos as soon as a source works, adds Smooth motion and seeking by dragging, and tidies the video player.

## What's in this release

**Sources**
- Videos start with the first source that works instead of waiting for every source, and switch to a clearly better one if it arrives in the first moments (never after you pick one). A source that played the title before still gets a few seconds to answer first.
- Songs and song downloads start from the first good match, and missing covers take the first one found.
- Edit details' cover search, a series' stream list and audiobooks' Find in other sources fill in as each source answers.

**Video player**
- An Effects button beside the speed button. Changing an effect while paused shows it on the paused frame at once.
- Smooth motion (Effects → Motion) makes in-between frames for smoother movement, and turns itself off on devices that can't keep up.
- Sound options (clearer dialogue, night mode, even loudness) are in the Effects sheet too.
- Seek by dragging (on by default on phones): a tap on the bar no longer jumps; drag it to see where you're going, with everything else out of the way. Swipe to seek turns off while it's on. Change it in Settings → Player.
- Sheets fit beside the navigation buttons in landscape, and the selected row stays in line with the others.
- Make default and Reset in the speed sheet show they were pressed, and Reset goes back to your default speed.
- Picture-in-picture shows the whole video, paused or playing, and the video comes back full size.
