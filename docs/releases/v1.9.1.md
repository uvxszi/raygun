Raygun 1.9.1 shows tables in books properly and fixes how Wikibooks' LaTeX book reads.

## What's in this release

**Books**
- Tables in books are tables: a header row, merged cells and a caption, with italics, code, formulas and pictures kept in their cells. Wide tables scroll sideways in their own box, in scrolled and paged reading.
- Read aloud reads a table a row at a time, each cell after its column's header.
- Italics, bold and inline code are kept in book text, list items keep their bullets and numbers, and a formula on its own line is drawn as one.
- Chapters downloaded from now on keep the source's own page and are laid out again when read, so later improvements reach them too.

**Sources**
- Wikibooks 1.1.0: names inside the LaTeX book's code examples are shown in italics instead of with two quote marks around them.
