Raygun 1.9.2 makes loading smooth, speeds up on-device voices, adds several sources at once, and brings more public-domain music and video.

## What's in this release

**Smoother**
- Covers, artwork, posters and icons fade in when they load, and fade from the old picture to the new one when they change. Pictures already seen show at once.
- Lists from sources ease in, loading placeholders fade into the content, and spinners only show when loading takes a moment.
- Dialogs, menus, panels and selection bars open with a short transition, and several jumps while loading are gone.

**Reading aloud**
- Piper and Kokoro voices are much faster: speech starts sooner, long sentences are split at pauses, and the next sentences are prepared while one plays. Voices downloaded before need downloading again.
- Voices not downloaded yet are listed with their size; choosing one downloads it in place and reads with it once it's here.
- "Letter I", "LETTER I." and other Roman numerals in titles are read as numbers.

**Sources**
- Sources → Add has a Several tab: one address per line, pasted, typed or from a text file. They're added one after another, with each line's progress, Retry and a summary at the end.
- New in Paraline, all without keys: Great 78 Project records over 100 years old, UCSB cylinder recordings, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, ESO and NOIRLab videos, and David Revoy's MiniFantasyTheater in Open webcomics.
