Raygun 1.7.0 brings Offline Mode and Data Saver, livestreams and a Quality button in the video player, clearer captions, search filters, labels on every icon button, and layouts that keep clear of the notch.

## What's in this release

**Offline Mode and Data Saver**
- Offline Mode (next to Incognito) stops all network use: downloads, cached data and your library keep working, queued downloads wait, and everything carries on when it's turned off.
- Data Saver uses lower video and music quality, asks fewer sources at once, and skips fetching ahead and background lookups. Each part can be switched on its own, or only on mobile data.

**Video player**
- Livestreams play from the live edge with a LIVE badge, and can be rewound within the stream's window. With AI subtitles, the picture can be held back so subtitles show at the right moment.
- A Quality button lists 4K down to 144p. It keeps to the current source when it can, and otherwise finds another source with that resolution. Tap it again to try the next source.
- Sources open on a simple list ordered by what works, quality and speed, with each option's size and speed beside it. The raw details are one tab away.
- Captions: colour changes apply at once, colours show as swatches, and custom colours are available. Your languages are listed first, and captions are sized to the picture in portrait. The delay can be typed in.
- Add a subtitle file, Transcribe with AI and Translate are buttons of their own. A track that's being made shows up and is picked automatically.
- Quality, captions and aspect ratio sit together at the bottom right. The shade runs smoothly edge to edge, and a quick seek no longer flashes the loading screen.

**Search**
- A Filters button for each kind: 18+ only, Explicit only, Playable only, Not in library.
- Switching tabs and pills answers at once, even with many sources.

**Everywhere**
- Holding an icon button on a phone, or resting the mouse on it, shows its name.
- In landscape, nothing sits under the notch or the navigation bar.
- Raygun checks for updates and offers new versions in a pop-up, with Later and Skip this version.
- Books without a cover get one drawn from their title and author.

**Music**
- Swiping the player bar brings the next song in from the opposite side.
- Choosing the phone speaker while headphones are plugged in now switches and stays switched. Devices shows where the sound really plays.

**Comics and sources**
- AI layers (upscale, restore, translate) swap pages in without a blank, and translated book text no longer blinks while it updates.
- More Mihon extensions convert, including ones that draw PDF pages.
