Raygun 2.0.1 connects to Navidrome, lets you tidy Home's Continue rows, follows your chosen language, and fixes and polishes the video player.

## What's in this release

**Navidrome**
- Add a Navidrome server in Sources → Add (its address, its web app's link or a share link). Browse its recently added, most played, starred and top-rated songs, genres, decades and internet radio, with synced lyrics from the server.
- Sync with Raygun, per server: stars and Favorites, playlists both ways, plays counted on the server, and the play queue kept on the server, so Feishin, Navic and Raygun stay in step.
- Songs from Subsonic and Navidrome servers now play at the quality you set instead of always the original.

**Home and settings**
- Continue reading, listening and watching, and Recently played: pick several items to remove them from Continue (they come back when you play them again), remove them from the library, mark them, download, pin or favorite them.
- Songs without a cover get one found automatically everywhere on Home, and again when sources are added, updated or reloaded.
- Settings → Appearance → App language: follow the system, or choose a language.
- The extension catalogue lists your language right after Many languages, and Search only shows "In your library" when something is there.

**Video**
- Quality: tapping the quality that's playing keeps it without reloading. Your preferred resolution's options come first, from every source.
- Raygun learns the sources you keep choosing and tries them first ("You usually watch from …").
- Second subtitles have everything the first have: a file, AI transcription and translation, their own delay, height and style.
- Subtitles stay with the track you picked when more arrive, and lines change exactly on time. Smart Subtitles can join lines that come close together.
- Effects change the paused picture at once, and a Custom pill shows when your effects match no preset. The custom subtitle colour shows as you pick it.
- Smooth motion is cleaner, with three models: Blend, Balanced and Sharp.
- Drawing a watermark box hides the controls, and only the watermark inside the box is changed.
- Pause works while the video is loading.
- A source's settings menu has a close button, and Back closes it first.

**Importing and barcodes**
- Screenshots can be opened, cropped, turned and removed before they're read.
- Barcode scanning reads for a moment and offers the most likely codes with what each finds, so a misread doesn't end in "nothing found".

**Fixes**
- Short songs count as played (and are scrobbled) even when the next one crossfades in.
- New sources' charts show at once instead of after an hour.
