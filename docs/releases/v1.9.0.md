Raygun 1.9.0 adds Answers in Search, live streams, pages for videos opened from links, a better read-aloud, translation choices and Google Translate, more Mihon extensions through hidden web views, and new open sources.

## What's in this release

**Answers**
- Search answers questions and calculations in a card at the top, with its own Answers pill: arithmetic, algebra, unit and currency conversion, dates and time zones, statistics and constants, offline where it can be.
- Facts from Wikidata, summaries from Wikipedia, definitions and translations, each with its source.
- Wolfram|Alpha can answer too, with your own AppID. Extensions can add other answer engines.

**Live streams**
- YouTube live streams play in the video player with the LIVE badge and rewind, when a YouTube source is installed.
- Twitch: browse what's live, categories and channels with your own Twitch app keys; streams and chat open in Twitch's own page.
- Live listings show a Live badge with the viewer count.

**Videos from links**
- A video opened from a link or a torrent gets its own page: its files and subtitles, a poster, Play, Download and Find what it is.
- The player has Sources and Quality for them too.

**Reading aloud**
- Voice, speed, pitch, volume and language are in the reader's own options, and change while it reads.
- Reading starts at the sentence you hold or tap, and the page follows the sentence being read.
- The book's language is used instead of guessing from quotes; Roman numerals in titles are read as numbers.

**Reading and translating**
- Translating a selection shows the detected language and the target, with a swap and a choice of translator.
- Google Translate is available as a provider with your own API key.
- Find in other sources wraps long titles, lets you edit the title and author, searches in other languages, and works for comics, videos, audiobooks and podcasts too.
- PDF books show their whole table of contents in both page and text mode.
- Code in books shows as code, and long links no longer make pages scroll sideways.

**Sources**
- More Mihon extensions run, including ones that need a hidden web view; sources behind a browser check say so and can be checked by hand.
- New in Paraline: Internet Archive's public-domain books, open webcomics (Sandra and Woo, turnoff.us), Creative Commons videos from PeerTube, and Wolfram|Alpha.

**Fixes**
- An app update, Force stop or swipe within the first seconds no longer counts as a crash.
- Formulas no longer show "&nbsp;" in place of spaces.
