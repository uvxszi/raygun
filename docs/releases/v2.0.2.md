Raygun 2.0.2 tidies the video player's quality lists and sheets, lowers the quality on its own when a video keeps stopping to load, and lets the screen sleep while a video is paused.

## What's in this release

**Quality**
- In Sources, each source lists its own qualities once each, and the one matching your preferred resolution carries a small Preferred tag and comes first.
- When a source has several links of the same quality, it shows once: tap it to play the best one, tap it again to choose another, with its size, codec and bit rate.
- The Quality button works the same way.
- In Automatic, a video that keeps stopping to load steps down a quality and says so ("Lowered to 720p to keep playing"); it never changes a quality you picked yourself.

**Sheets**
- Smart audio is four small buttons with icons at the top of the Audio sheet (Dialogue, Night, Even volume, Level scenes); hold one to read what it does. Adding an audio file is a small button by the title.
- The audio, subtitle and second-subtitle delays sit in one row with their buttons.

**Screen**
- The screen sleeps as usual while a video is paused. To keep it on, turn on Keep the screen on while paused in the video player's settings.
